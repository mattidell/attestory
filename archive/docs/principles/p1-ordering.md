I'd order by dependency — each principle should be readable using only vocabulary the ones before it established. That gives four movements:

**I. The workspace and its state** — what the record *is*.

1. **No state exists outside the workspace** — establishes the single noun everything else refers to. It has to go first; every other principle says "workspace" and assumes this.
2. **Every change is complete** — how that state changes.
3. **Nothing is gated** — how that state is seen. (2 and 3 are the consistency and access halves of wholeness, in that order because visibility of incompleteness presumes incompleteness is well-defined.)
4. **Every value carries its explanation** — what that state owes. It was your #1, and it's arguably the soul of the project, but it *reads* better fourth: "grounded in the record" needs the record defined first. If you want it first as a thesis statement, that's a legitimate rhetorical choice — lead with the why — but then it borrows vocabulary on credit.

**II. Facts and authority** — how truth enters.

5. **Facts are peers, not document children** — defines fact and assertion.
6. **Judgment lives with the facts** — a species of assertion.
7. **The system proposes; you sign** — the gate between system output and fact.

**III. Structure and derivation** — the machinery. This is the cluster, in the memo's dependency order:

8. **Schema is canon** — the states.
9. **No new noun without a schema** — pulled forward from your #13 to sit directly after its stock/flow partner. Separating them costs more than any thematic gain.
10. **Derivation logic is legible data** — the terms.
11. **Computation publishes facts** — the execution.
12. **Every run leaves a record** — the evidence.
13. **Derived facts carry their contract** — the authority. It consumes 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, and 12, so it must come last of the cluster — and it's a natural crescendo, since it's where both signature paths meet and the jurat lives.

**IV. The perimeter** — who the system serves.

14. **Synthetic by default, personal data quarantined**
15. **The user controls the context** — closes the list where it started, with the user as the sole authority, now over attention rather than facts.

One alternative worth naming: swap movements I and II to lead with "Facts are peers" and "The system proposes; you sign" — the human-authority principles — if the audience is users rather than builders. For a design document, dependency order above is the one I'd publish.
