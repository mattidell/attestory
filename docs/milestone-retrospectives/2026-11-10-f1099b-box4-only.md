# Retrospective: Form 1099-B Backup Withholding Without a Sale (2025)

Milestone key: f1099b-box4-only. Plan:
`docs/phases/engine-breadth/milestones/f1099b-box4-only.md`.
Closed 2026-11-10.

## What happened

Core v84 counted a recorded box 4 amount on a Form 1099-B statement with
no sale, but asked the amount only of statements with a sale, so a filer
whose only box 4 came from an open short was never asked and line 25b
could publish short. Core v85 asks every return one yes/no and, on yes, one
total, added to line 25b.

P0 found that nothing closes the set of Form 1099-B statements and no
surface records a statement without a sale, so a per-statement question
would have needed a recording change. The return-level shape is content
only. It newly asks 91 of the 298 recorded returns; Track 0 replayed all
91 with the answer "no" and every number came back.

The fresh review found no wrong number. Its should-fix: the questions told
the filer to leave out box 4 entered "with its sales", but an amount stays
on a statement after its sale is removed and still counts, so that filer
could enter it twice. The repair widened the wording to any box 4 already
entered.

## What worked

- Measuring the restore, not asserting it: the 91 reran with one added
  answer and matched the recorded values.
- One grok builder session through P0, Track 0, Track 1 and the repair:
  about $24 in total, the lowest of the recent Form 1099-B milestones.
- The charter's rule against duplicate act lists kept the live grid to
  unique runs from the start.

## What to change

- A question that tells the filer what to exclude is a claim about every
  path that can leave a value behind. Walk the late-member traces against
  the wording, not only against the numbers.
