# Retrospective: Every Line Speaks Plainly (2025)

Milestone key: plain-line-texts. Plan:
`docs/phases/engine-breadth/milestones/plain-line-texts.md`.
Closed 2026-11-18. Core v96, registry v94, release v94. 61 content
successors and one additive entry in the package validator's line 2b
successor-graph table. No schema, kernel, projection or page change.

## What happened

The milestone opened on a parked note that kept texts still said
"derived finding" or "closed-empty". P0 stopped at once: most engine
words on a saved page come from the page itself (support-trace captions,
badges, banners), not content. The foreman narrowed the scope to
content-supplied text and parked the page wording behind
blocked-sale-pages. P0b took a census of the 775 recorded lists and
drafted 57 replacements, then stopped on the package validator, which
pins line 2b's form field at v6 for every core from v68; the foreman
authorized a v96 entry on the v68 precedent. P0c built the scratch core
and replayed it strictly.

Track 0 (a fresh context) rewrote 22 of the 57: the projection draws the
"computed zero" explanation on returns with no record of the form as
well as on amounts that round to zero, and P0c's sentences claimed
records the return did not have. Track 1 published it. The fresh review
was ready but found four drawn texts the term filter had missed,
including line 6a's "computed zero for Form 1040 line 6a" on 693 of 699
pages. The repair read all 243 distinct drawn texts instead of
filtering, replaced 12 more, and parked two sale reasons.

Dispatch changed three times on budgets: Codex (P0-P0c, then its limit),
agy Gemini 4 Argon (Track 0, review), agy Sonnet (Track 1, until its
five-hour budget), grok (repair, until its balance ran out after the
replay), a Claude Sonnet sub-agent (repair checks). Every unit committed
before its budget ended, so no handoff lost work.

## What worked

- Stopping at the page boundary in P0 rather than drifting into another
  foreman's page.
- A fresh context for Track 0. The author of 57 sentences read them as
  meant; the adversary built the states where they were false.
- The strict classifier and mutation controls carried unchanged across
  five builders and three model families.

## What to change

- A term list is a proxy for "engine language" and missed the commonest
  phrase. When the population of drawn texts is small (243 here), read
  it whole in P0.
- Check whether a validator pins the version a content successor
  replaces before drafting it; P0b found the line 2b gate only at build.
- Expect budget exhaustion mid-unit and charter "commit after each item"
  everywhere; it made every handoff cheap.
