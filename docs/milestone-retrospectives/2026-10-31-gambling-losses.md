# Retrospective — Gambling Losses (2025)

## Result

- **Gambling losses compute on core v67.** A standard-deduction filer with
  losses is no longer refused and gets the same figures; an itemizer
  deducts losses up to winnings on Schedule A line 16, and losses can decide
  whether to itemize.
- **The filed attachment is incomplete.** The Schedule A attachment does not
  itemize line 16, so for a gambling-loss itemizer it lists less than line
  12e uses. The attachment shape cannot express a part that is conditional or
  limited; that is an owner question.

## What the evidence changed

- **The cost of honesty about "no new question" was the attachment.** Every
  way to put line 16 on Schedule A with today's attachment schemas would make
  every itemizer without a W-2G confirm a losses family. Keeping returns
  without gambling unchanged left the attachment short.
- **A named refusal needs an engine change.** Block codes are a closed set;
  a rule-specific name would need a runner code and a schema successor. The
  refusal works; its reason lives in the rule's notes.

## Process

- Track 1 stopped twice on its own stop conditions, and each stop came with
  a measured account of what the engine allows. Both were dispositioned the
  same hour.
