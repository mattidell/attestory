# Retrospective — An Unresolved Form 1099-K Stops the Bottom Line (2025)

## Result

- **On core v60 the bottom line waits for every Form 1099-K.** While any
  Form 1099-K is unresolved (a nonzero box 4, an unanswered circumstance
  question, or any loss reason other than a supported personal-item loss),
  or while the filer has not confirmed every Form 1099-K is recorded,
  Schedule 1 line 10 blocks and names the cause. Form 1040 lines 8 through
  24 and 32 through 37 wait behind it; line 25b still publishes.
- **A resolved Form 1099-K, or none, computes exactly as on v59.** One
  successor rule and form field (Schedule 1 line 10 v2); no engine, schema or
  expression-operation change; no new question on adoption, because v59
  already asks the 1099-K closure through the Schedule 1 top entry.
- **Core v59 and earlier keep the gap**, pinned by tests.
- Forms 1099-DIV and 1099-R recorded without a box 4 answer are tested beside
  the 1099-INT case.

## What the evidence changed

- **The gap was one missing link.** Schedule 1 line 10 read only
  unemployment and the Part I absence answers; no rule in v59 read the 1099-K
  condition. The top entry and the attachment blocked and nothing waited on
  them.
- **P0's hand trace was short twice, in the safe direction.** Line 33 also
  blocks, because line 32's refundable credits read AGI; and each lower line
  names other inputs the block stops. The tests compare against the run, and
  name exact missing lists only where the cause is one link.
- **"Unresolved" was not the only way to be unknown.** The final review found
  that a Form 1099-K family nobody closed still published a refund. Line 10
  now requires the closure too.
