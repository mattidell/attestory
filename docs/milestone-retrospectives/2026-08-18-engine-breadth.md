# Retrospective — Engine Breadth Phase

## What differed from the plan

The phase began as a frontier-driven sequence that would retire one named
honest block at a time. It did that roughly twenty times across breadth slices
and their necessary prerequisite repairs, widening capital gains, interest,
adjustments, benefits, deductions, schedules, and downstream return
composition without weakening the existing boundaries.

The frontier did not run empty. General Form 1116, other Schedule D sources,
other K-1 boxes, and noncovered Form 8949 transactions remain live. The owner
closed the phase by judgment: another row would add coverage through a shape
already repeatedly proved, not establish a new engine capability.

The noncovered-basis Track 0 also demonstrated why a paper milestone cannot be
resumed merely because its prerequisite later lands. ADR-0066 independently
settled its consumer-dependency substrate, superseding the proposed
ADR-0063/0064/0065 drafts before ratification. Only the evaluation-ordering
analysis and traversal-totality proof remain reusable.

## What it cost

Seven-to-nine hand-maintained version allowlists produced a silent latent
defect in four consecutive milestones. The defect class survived ordinary
tests because a schema could be registered at most sites while missing from
one capability-specific set. One instance was fixed by review and then
re-broken during curation of the same PR.

ADR-0066 Decision 7 closed the wholly unregistered-schema case. It did not
close the partially registered case. The capability-table consolidation was
therefore correctly classified as cross-cutting hardening and deferred in a
filed, scoped, deliberately unselected plan instead of being smuggled into a
breadth milestone.

Publication curation carried a second structural cost. Curation was permitted
to modify engine files in PR #178 and produced a net regression after the
working implementation had already repaired the relevant version set. The
final review caught it; the prior green suite did not.

## Follow-ups

- Re-cut noncovered basis only from ADR-0066 and only after the owner settles
  C3: whether a closure gate may pass while recording counterexamples to its
  own bar.
- Keep the rule-artifact/attachment-rule capability-table consolidation filed
  as hardening. Its trigger is explicit owner selection, not another local
  allowlist omission.
- Carry every phase deferral ledger by path from the roadmap close record;
  none is silently discharged by phase closure.
- Keep fact entry and filing visible as product gaps. Engine Breadth changed
  computation coverage, not the user's JSON entry workflow or filing ability.

## What should change in the next plan

Curation should be provably tree-identical outside `docs/`. If a curation must
change engine files, that change needs its own scoped review against the exact
post-curation tree before publication. A passing suite is not evidence that a
partially registered capability set stayed complete.
