# Retrospective — Form 1099-DIV Box 12 to Form 1040 Line 2a

## What differed from the plan

- Track 0 settled the smallest complete boundary: one independent Form
  1099-DIV box-12 family, plus explicit absence authority for every other
  tax-exempt source, premium adjustment, and excluded downstream dependency.
  Box 13 remained a companion absence/zero authority; a nonzero value blocks
  without introducing Form 6251 or AMT computation.
- The residual successor removed box 12 while preserving the historical
  residual bundle and published package bytes. Package admission rejects a
  mixed historical-residual and successor-family graph, so one statement
  cannot contribute box 12 through both shapes.
- The final rebase incorporated the ratified Schedule D carryover work. The
  selected core package advanced to v17, the published registry to v12, the
  release to v10, and the adoption to v17, with artifact-package.v14. The
  semantic ledger captured 13 member changes, 6 entrypoint changes, 2
  admitted-schema changes, and no input-binding or composition-obligation
  changes. Verification and the original-base negative control both returned
  exit 0. A second generator run was reproducible; downstream checksum
  changes were generated from the first package-version successor rather than
  semantic drift.
- The independent review first found one production-path defect: the live
  coordinator did not install the domain box-13 companion-presence pair.
  One bounded findings-only repair wired that pair into the live path. The
  author-independent re-review returned **READY** and confirmed missing
  companions block, null/zero companions publish, and nonzero box 13 blocks
  without Form 6251.
- Two package-exclusivity probes remain pre-existing synthetic fixture hygiene
  gaps: they fail earlier on missing quantity vocabulary/member reachability
  instead of reaching their intended mixed/raw rejection codes. They were
  unchanged by the repair and do not weaken the bounded production claim.

## Result

The engine now has a bounded, production-shaped synthetic path for 2025
returns whose only tax-exempt-interest source is one or more nonnegative Form
1099-DIV box-12 amounts, with box 13 absent or zero and all excluded source and
downstream declarations closed. The complete total is reported on Form 1040
line 2a, with citation and authority in explanation and the existing
presentation surface. It remains reported-but-not-directly-taxable: line 2a
does not enter line 9 or taxable-income arithmetic.

The result does not claim general tax-exempt-interest support. Form 1099-INT
and Form 1099-OID tax-exempt sources, unreported interest, premium adjustments,
box 13/AMT treatment, state returns, and excluded downstream consumers remain
honestly blocked or outside the selected graph. Existing dividend, taxable
interest, Schedule B, and Schedule D behavior remains covered by focused
regressions.
