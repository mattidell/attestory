# Retrospective — Student Loan Circumstance Association

## What differed from the plan

The initial unit sought a recorded statement-to-borrowing connection in place
of the earlier experiment's stipulated link. It first needed repairs to
scheduling, exact category types, and saved evidence so a relationship could
survive every relevant consumer path. The accepted reader demonstration stayed
experimental, and the tax consequence remained a separate decision.

The result is an approved partial milestone: a reviewed relationship correction
route and neutral saved consumer, without selecting mixed-period treatment or
producing a deduction.

## What it cost

The work expanded through several small evidence and repair units. Repeatedly,
a value or type appeared correct at one producer and disappeared or changed
meaning at the next consumer. Tests that stopped at the intermediate map missed
those defects. The useful regressions entered through ordinary package
admission, marshalling, both runners, durable recovery and saved reading.

A changed statement amount did not say whether borrowing inclusion changed.
The correction route therefore had to record scope explicitly and update the
relationship's current standing. Multiple ActLog appends made interruption
behavior observable; the tested order ends obsolete support before a failed
answer can appear saved.

## Follow-ups

- When a production statement-update path is selected, enforce correction
  scope on every accepted write, including routes outside this experiment.
- When a production multi-act recorder is selected, test interrupted-prefix
  recovery or continuation; the bounded fail-closed cases are not atomicity.
- When a bounded tax producer is selected, make its treatment and automation
  scope explicit, then execute its amount, saved meaning and refusal cases.
  Keep the worksheet result and any person-supplied scoped value distinguishable
  from the neutral relationship evidence.
- When reader integration becomes product work, decide final wording and
  placement from saved evidence instead of treating the experimental page as
  accepted text.

## What should change in the next plan

Name one user capability, the entry paths that can change it, and the consumer
that will rely on it. Require a recovered-result test at each information-loss
boundary. Classify later work as implementation, open product choice, or a
possible person-supplied value before treating it as a build promise.
