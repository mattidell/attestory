# Retrospective — Tax Concept Derivation Phase

Closed 2026-09-17 by owner direction as a bounded demonstration of viability.
This is not a claim of broad tax coverage, a second production tax concept, or
a completed user-facing explanation journey.

## What the phase established

A document's report, a person's ordinary account of their circumstances, a
rule's tax inference, and a return's presentation can remain distinct while
working together. The nominee-interest vertical demonstrates this in the
production engine: a current allocation changes a bounded return result
without rewriting the payer's report or asking the person for the tax
conclusion. The result has a declared rule and recoverable lineage.

A contrasting student-loan-interest experiment tested the same division of
labor outside interest-income adjustment. One ordinary adverse fact could
support a rule-owned consequence through real engine machinery. Its candidate
artifacts were disposable and its statement-to-loan relationship stipulated;
it did not deliver another production route. This is evidence of method
transfer, not proof of generality.

## What to carry forward

- **Use ordinary facts to locate tax consequences, not to make the person
  supply them.** A source document is evidence of what was reported; neither
  its presence nor a person's answer automatically settles every tax premise.
- **Keep the evidence rung visible.** A paper model, a disposable execution,
  an adopted rule, and a reader-facing result answer different questions.
  Success at one rung does not silently satisfy the next.
- **Separate the concept from executable coverage.** A bounded result can be
  useful without redefining the tax concept as the subset the engine handles.
  Unknown, unsupported, and supported-negative states must not collapse.
- **Trace the consumer.** A computed number alone does not show that the
  adopted rule owns it, the return uses it, or a reader can understand why it
  stands. Each claim needs evidence at the layer where it matters.

## Open boundary

The original fresh-reader explanation criterion remains partial. Broader
question routing and the user journey are deferred to a later phase by owner
direction, not counted as delivered. The student-loan production premises,
later-year basis composition, wider tax coverage, and nominee-path robustness
gaps remain bounded in the [phase roadmap](../phases/tax-concept-derivation/tax-concept-derivation-roadmap.md#phase-close--2026-09-17).

No next phase is selected here. Its purpose should come from the owner's next
intention, informed by this result rather than inherited from the unfinished
roadmap item.
