# Retrospective — A Blocked Line Names Its Question (2025)

## Result

- **No product change; closed on P0.** A line that blocks because a family
  is unconfirmed shows a generic "blocked" text, a code, and "contribute the
  missing dependency". When a subtotal is the root, no page line names it at
  all.
- **The wording does not exist.** None of the 50 closures declares a
  question; their titles are written for the engine, not the filer.
- **The fix is costed.** A `source-closure-mapping.v3` schema with
  `closure_question`, 50 successors, admission edits in four derivation
  files including the live coordinator, and a projector reason in a shape the
  saved model and page already accept.

## What the evidence changed

- **The last four milestones made this matter.** Each added honest closure
  blocks; together they make "confirm the family" the most common thing a
  filer has to do, and the product never says so in words.
- **The coordinator filters mapping schemas by exact version.** A v3 mapping
  would be dropped silently, not refused. That is worth knowing before any
  closure-mapping successor.

## Process

- One Sonnet 5.5 P0 (about 10 minutes); its stop conditions held and the
  foreman closed rather than widen the ceiling.
