## What differed from the plan

The readiness inventory said package v8 selected line-2b field content v2.
Inspection showed that v8 selected v1 while v2 was published but unselected.
The plan was corrected before implementation so package v9 selects the new v3
successor without mutating or silently promoting either historical version.

No implementation repair was needed. The independent integrated review
accepted the complete positive, blocking, lifecycle, package, explanation, and
presentation matrix on its first pass.

## What it cost

The integrated Builder ran for about 24 minutes and reported 78 tool calls.
Most of the unusually large diff came from additive schemas, package/release
citizens, and generated presentation fixtures needed to prove one coherent
source-to-presentation path.

The streamlined one-Builder, one-Reviewer loop avoided track handoffs and a
repair round. Its main cost was concentrating schema, runtime, content,
fixtures, and package-route reasoning in one build packet.

## Follow-ups

- Select other K-1 forms or boxes only when their own source and consumer
  boundary is named; Form-1065 box-5 support is not a general K-1 claim.
- Select market discount separately when its securities-source authority can
  be bounded without importing a general transaction domain.
- Select subtractive adjustments only with an explicit arithmetic and
  explanation contract.

## What should change in the next plan

Verify selected package versions, not merely published content versions, in
the readiness inventory. Keep a single integrated packet for similarly bounded
successor work, but identify generated fixture volume separately so review can
focus early on contract and runtime seams.
