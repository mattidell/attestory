# Lessons — One Home Loan, More Than One Interest Statement

Two forms about the same loan do not necessarily describe different payments.
Ask about the relationship between the exact forms before adding their amounts;
neither matching labels nor matching amounts settles the question.

An answer may remain in the person's history while no longer applying to the
current documents. Test the whole path from recorded evidence to usable inputs,
and check that neither ordinary assertions nor malformed intermediate readings
can stand in for supported relationships. Distinguish the person's uncertainty
from the system's inability to establish whether an earlier answer still applies.

A prerequisite can block a fresh calculation without appearing in its retained
dependency history. Check whether intermediate findings and final amounts become
stale when their actual support changes. A restored source needs fresh support;
it must not revive earlier findings or completeness claims.

When another milestone lands during development, reproduce the intended package
changes on the complete newer base. Keep the publication recipe independent of
disposable working commits so a fresh checkout can reproduce the result.

Broader mortgage allocation, refinancing, more homes or borrowings, and the
higher grandfathered limit need concrete future consumers and designs. Browser
evaluation remains separate from saved-model and bounded renderer checks.
