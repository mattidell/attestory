# Retrospective — Reported Interest to Tax Concept Vertical Slice

## What differed from the plan

Track 0's stop-before-prototype clause fired on paper plus the incumbent and
was treated as an answer. It was not: the incumbent cannot be given the
ordinary purchase circumstance, so it cannot decide whether an item-level
determination is necessary. Track 1 had to run.

The comparison then spent most of its cost repairing evidence-ceiling defects
rather than changing the headline. Six exhibit iterations were needed before
source-report support, tax authority, tax coverage, recorded reconstruction,
and fact-version currentness were separately recoverable. Arithmetic never
discriminated. No production representation was selected.

## What it cost

Six exhibits (`it1`–`it6`) and three retained independent reviews, each
forcing a bounded successor. Recurring classes: source support stored as tax
authority or coverage; later-year reconstruction labelled current in modes
that cannot decide currentness; access grants described as more durable than
the in-memory objects actually granted. The last repair renamed task 5 to
recorded-partition recovery and left task 6 as currentness. Unamended score
counts did not change.

Orientation also misfired: `current_role` containing "independent" selected
a clean-room block for a successor repair.

## Follow-ups

- **Later disposition of the same obligation.** Trigger: the owner selects the
  later-year basis-consequence frontier. Ask whether the existing fact, rule,
  artifact, and provenance model can carry the basis drop to that consumer.
- **Split-state product consequence.** Trigger: a later-year consumer exists
  and the basis amount can stand while a current partition explanation cannot.
  Recompute, retain as historical, withhold, or name another task. Do not
  reopen whether a displaced producer supports a current explanation.
- **`citation.v1` has no Treasury Regulation family.** Trigger: a production
  proposition whose best support is regulatory.

## What should change in the next plan

Use `PROJECT_PLANNING.md`, Frontier Reduction and Direct-Build Routing. Fix
the later-year consumer and failure test on paper first. Prototype only if
materially different representations would change that consumer. Do not treat
an unamended fixture as a capability granted to the later-year reader. Do not
call recorded reconstruction current. If a role string contains "independent",
do not let that word select clean-room orientation for a repair.
