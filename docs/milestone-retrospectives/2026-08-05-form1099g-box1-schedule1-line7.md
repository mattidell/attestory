## Result

The engine has a bounded, production-shaped synthetic path for 2025 individual
returns with one or more nonnegative Form 1099-G box-1 unemployment amounts
(box 4 null or zero, no repayment, no other Schedule 1 income). The amount
flows Form 1099-G box 1 → Schedule 1 line 7 → Schedule 1 line 10 → Form 1040
line 8 → Form 1040 line 9 and the existing taxable-income and tax graph, with
correction lifecycle, package resolution, explanation, and presentation.

It does not claim general Schedule 1 support, box-4 withholding / line 25b,
other Form 1099-G boxes as computed income, repayments, or payments/filing.
W-2, interest, dividends, capital-gain, Schedule B, Schedule D, and line 2a
behavior remain covered by focused regressions on the final base.
