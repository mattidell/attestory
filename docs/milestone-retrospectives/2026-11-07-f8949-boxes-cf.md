# Retrospective: Sales Without a Form 1099-B (2025)

Milestone key: f8949-boxes-cf. Plan:
`docs/phases/engine-breadth/milestones/f8949-boxes-cf.md`.
Closed 2026-11-07.

## What happened

P0 found that core v79 published a wage-only return when a box C or F sale
was left unrecorded and every Form 8949 boundary was answered "no", and
that a box E sale missing its basis or proceeds amount computed silently.
It proposed a content-only successor with a second grouped question, so
that v79's answers keep their meaning. The foreman decided the design,
accepted the four v79 returns it newly blocks, and added the
missing-amount check to lines 2 and 9. Track 0 found seven more
counterexamples on the decided design. These were shared transaction
records, unchecked agreement between a sale and its amounts, and answers
admitted with nothing recorded. It fixed six by content and refused the
seventh by answer, then passed all six rows. Track 1 built core v80,
registry v78 and release v78, byte-identical to the prototype. The fresh
review matched every hand-computed line. It found a question worded wider
than its proposition, a red fast-lane budget, a blocked reason that blamed
the wrong term, and a sale counted twice across terms. The repair fixed
them in place on the unmerged v80. No engine, schema or recording change
was needed.

## What worked

- Hand-computed cases and attachment dispositions were in the charters
  from P0, following the noncovered-sales retrospective. The review
  found no wrong expected value.
- Track 0 again caught real defects before the build, this time on a
  design P0 had called complete.
- Pinning the generated content byte for byte against the prototype made
  the in-place repair of an unmerged package safe to check.

## What to change

- An input question's words must be checked against its proposition
  when the question is first written. The in-class answer had no input
  question on v79, and the new wording was wider than the proposition it
  asks about.
- A projection that pins only values and questions cannot tell two block
  paths apart. Pin each blocked line's reason and its row's (disposition,
  code, missing) from the first live test.
- Two evidence folders each held a `track0.py`, which clashed when both
  modules loaded in one process. Load evidence harnesses under unique
  module names.
