# Retrospective: The Earned Income Credit for a Head of Household (2025)

Milestone key: hoh-eic. Plan:
`docs/phases/engine-breadth/milestones/hoh-eic.md`.
Closed 2026-11-19. Core v99, registry v97, release v97: one new answer
and two input questions (not married; the existing separated-spouse
fact), `rule.eic-answer.not-married`, `rule.eic-status-class` v2,
`rule.eic-gate-result` v3 and five Step 4 answer-rule v3 successors. No
engine, schema, projection, page or kernel change.

## What happened

The hoh-qualifying-person P0 noticed that every head of household with
no qualifying child got a $0 earned income credit, although the 2025
instructions stop only a married one. P0 (a Claude Opus builder) measured
537 and 236 lost at low incomes and found nothing on the record that
tells an unmarried head of household from a "considered unmarried" one,
so it designed one question. The foreman added two ways to be unmarried
the draft left out (an earlier death, an annulment), and folded in the
separated-spouse answer's missing question. A Claude Sonnet builder
published it.

The fresh review (Claude Opus) found the "no" did not stop: a married
head of household still had to give five more credit answers, two of
which have no question, so a return that computed a $350 refund on v98
blocked with no sentence. The probes had pre-answered those two, and every
recorded list is single, so no evidence could see it. A Sonnet builder
added five answer-rule successors that skip them after a "no", and made
two fast-lane tests fast by reading the published corpus once.

## What worked

- Reading the draft question against the sources before Track 1. It
  caught the missing earlier death and annulment.
- A review question that asked who is newly asked something. It found
  the five extra answers.

## What to change

- A probe that pre-fills answers cannot show which answers a change
  newly requires. Leave each answer the change could add unanswered in
  at least one probe.
- When every recorded list has one filing status, the replay says nothing
  about the others; say so in the evidence rather than counting passes.
