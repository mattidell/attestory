# Retrospective — Investment Basis Concept and Coverage Model

## What differed from the plan

The plan allowed the milestone to close either by naming a first
production vertical or as an explicit partial result. It closed as the
partial result, and that is a legitimate outcome rather than a shortfall:
the basis concept, its representative cases, and its coverage are
established, while the A-versus-B representation choice is deferred
because no concrete consumer behaves differently under either shape.

## What it cost

Most of the cost was not in producing the domain model, coverage matrix,
or propositions. It was in establishing that claims about *what the
committed system already does* were true. Claims about runtime identity
paths, inferred from reading code, twice reached architectural
conclusions that a single run of an existing fixture disproved.

A second cost came from applying a process instrument that did not fit.
The Prototype Economic Gate was scored repeatedly against a question that
had no concrete proposition or mechanism to score — the results would not
stabilize, because the object being scored was an unspecified family of
possible mechanisms rather than a proposition.

## Follow-ups

- **The four composition gaps** — absent `purchase_price`/
  `acquisition_costs` vocabulary; no basis-origin producer keyed by the
  acquisition identity; no content-declared per-acquisition publication
  path; no declared traversal from an acquisition-keyed origin, through
  the association record, to the sibling pairing-scoped consequence.
  *Trigger:* any milestone selecting a first basis-lifecycle production
  vertical.
- **RC3 / RC4 machinery accommodation.** *Trigger:* selecting an OID or
  premium-amortization case. Resolving it requires a bounded
  instantiation, not inference from the evaluator's operation list.
- **RC5 reconciliation.** *Trigger:* broker-reported basis must be
  reconciled with a product-derived adjustment for the same instrument.
- **RC6 allocation.** *Trigger:* a lot-level disposition consumer exists.
- **The A-versus-B choice.** *Trigger:* a consumer that must read a
  composed adjusted basis. The Later-Year Basis Reuse Test is the context
  in which such a consumer first appears and the choice can be tested; it
  may discriminate the candidates or may show no material difference.

## What should change in the next plan

**Reproduce a runtime path with an available production-shaped fixture
before letting a claim about it drive an architectural conclusion.**
Reading the code is not equivalent, and the cheaper check is the one that
settles it.

**Before scoring a decision against the Prototype Economic Gate, confirm
there is a concrete proposition and a named consumer behavior to score.**
An unselected family of possible mechanisms is not a scoreable object; a
score that will not stabilize is evidence the wrong instrument is in
hand. Where no consumer distinguishes the alternatives, Frontier
Reduction and Direct-Build Routing already prescribes the answer: stay at
paper, record the missing discriminator, defer.

**The four-state cadence — rival evidence, disposable integration
evidence, consolidated contracts, clean production build — remains
untested,** because this milestone stopped at paper and never reached
integration or production. Carry it into the next milestone that actually
builds through all four states.
