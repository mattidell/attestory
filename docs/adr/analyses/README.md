# Plain-Language ADR Analyses

These companion documents explain an ADR in ordinary language: what changes,
why it is needed, what it protects or enables, and what it deliberately does
not do. They are written for a reader who should not need to reconstruct the
contract from schemas, code, or prototype evidence.

They are explanatory only. The linked ADR remains the normative decision record;
when the two appear to differ, the ADR governs.

For every new or materially revised ADR, create
`docs/adr/analyses/<adr-number>-<slug>.md` and add a relative link to it near
the top of the ADR. Historical ADRs need not be retrofitted solely to meet this
new protocol; add companions when they are materially revisited.
