# Independent review — connected mortgage design and feasibility experiment

Audience: Reviewer.

## Purpose and object

Review P1's connected paper account and proposed focused experiment before
we spend on implementation. Does the candidate preserve what each report
says and what the person affirms, and will the experiment expose the proposed
mechanism's failure rather than merely produce a plausible display?

Object at launch: the current
`docs/phases/tax-circumstance-breadth/milestones/mortgage-statement-borrowing-evidence/p1-connected-account.md`
and the active plan's P1 direction. P0's reviewed account and diagnostic are
established background at their stated scope, not proof that P1 works.
The charter/pointer commit is routing, not additional product work under review.

## First return — readiness for the experiment

Attack these material questions together:

- Does an annual numerical upper bound have the right meaning? Within-limit
  support is different from a loose bound above the limit, an actual dated
  observation above a limit, and a source contradicting the confirmed bound.
  Check changed filing status without inventing a new answer from the old one.
- Are ordinary statements scoped by their actual proposition? Explicitly
  shared loan-wide support may cover linked reports; equal report-local
  values cannot borrow one another's authority. Check all required inputs and
  pins, not just principal. Return-wide filing status remains distinct.
- Does the candidate handle the report set and route transitions honestly?
  Withdrawing a shared answer or removing a report cannot hide still-current
  contrary/unresolved information behind a count-based legacy fallback.
  Preserve genuine legacy-only single-report and closed-empty behavior.
  A closed-empty Form 1098 family establishes zero reported Box 1 members,
  not the absence of all home debt; remaining loan claims do not alone defeat
  that zero or create a new no-activity prerequisite.
- Are known zero, unsupported coverage and unresolved support distinguished?
  Do not let one determined report-local outcome accidentally authorize a
  mixed-payment subset sum beyond this milestone's boundary.
- Does the mechanism fit exact existing contracts? The F1098 v1 family
  authorizes `tax.us.2025.schedule-a.line8a`, not any newly named raw subtotal.
  Inspect actual collection-authority admission and single-producer/cardinality
  expectations. A precedent must share those properties, not just a shape.
- Is the proposed provenance carrier real? `SourceFact` has one finding id.
  A scoped view cannot gain a second dependency merely through prose or
  fabricated keys. Check declared intermediate findings or another concrete
  carrier against actual dispatch, scheduling and live material construction.
- Does lifecycle wording separate recorded assertions, same-run publications,
  stale saved revision and a fresh run? No new cross-run persistence caller or
  rewrite of old output is implied by this milestone.
- Can the proposed experiment discriminate? Require a valid positive case
  and independently mutated negatives; don't let a second-property count,
  contradictory origination dates or several simultaneous failures mask the
  source join being evaluated. Identify exactly what is fixture-supplied,
  proposed code/content, real machinery and unproven production behavior.
  Raw account/link assertions may be supplied. The intermediate derived
  findings must actually execute; a ready-made intermediate is a stand-in
  for the capability under examination. Check the producer-removal kill
  control and a concrete proposed rule/payload instance.

Verify load-bearing source or contract text, not the author's summaries. Use
primary sources for any new tax proposition. Inspect targeted code; do not
rerun P0 or a suite to confirm unchanged observations. This first return is
a design/probe-readiness review, not production acceptance or full Track 0
closure. Do not demand completed production tests at the paper-design stage.

Write one report at
`docs/reviews/mortgage-statement-borrowing/p1-independent-review.md`:
READY FOR BOUNDED EXPERIMENT or REPAIR REQUIRED, with material findings,
actual evidence/ceiling, and any genuine new owner choice. Distinguish a defect
in the account from an existing limitation the proposed experiment tests.

## Following the experiment

On explicit Foreman continuation under this same review unit, inspect its
actual implementation and output against the above claims, including provenance
and integration limitations. Recheck the experiment and changed conclusions,
not all unchanged P0 or legal material. Append that evidence disposition to
the report; do not silently turn readiness to experiment into production
readiness. Track 0 and any required contract/implementation readiness remain
separate explicit gates.
