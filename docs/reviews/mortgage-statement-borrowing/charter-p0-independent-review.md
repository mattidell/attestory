   conditional Part II calculation; do not require a general average-balance

2. Trace the exact package/release and the diagnostic setup. Changes made
   before a synthetic log is initialized must be disclosed, and validation,
   real durable writes and real coordinator execution must not be bypassed.
   Historical-route observations are not latest-package evidence.
3. Separate the offered questions, fixture-selected normalized answers,
   real recorded acts, declared defaults, derived findings and reloaded saved
   output. No human usability test, raw-form ingestion, institutional
   verification or obtained same-loan relationship follows from those steps.
4. Check each independent one-report restriction. In the two-report case,
   review/save refusals and calculation dispositions are distinct. Confirm
   both exact-key saves refuse without changing revision. In the over-limit
   case, ensure the actual nonzero principal reaches its comparison; read
   real reason, amount and saved status rather than infer one from another.

   recording, source emission/binding, subject dispatch, aggregation, closure
   authority and saved display. The family v1 already authorizes a Box 1 sum;
   current singleton handling does not alone prove a family/schema successor
   is needed. Existing student-loan and sale mechanisms are evidence of
   capabilities, not proof of mortgage identity or debt treatment.
6. Check whether lifecycle statements are established by code, earlier tests,
   or new execution, and whether the next unresolved product choices are
