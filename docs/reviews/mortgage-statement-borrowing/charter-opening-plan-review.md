# Independent review — opening same-borrowing plan

Audience: Reviewer.

## Question

Is the opening account specific enough to begin a bounded P0 investigation,
without silently selecting a loan representation, debt formula or wider tax
feature? Review readiness to investigate, not readiness to implement.

The opening plan is committed separately from this charter. Review its goal,
actions, questions and cases alongside the prior mortgage/multiple-statement
outcomes. No production code or new tax calculation has been built.

## Focus

- Does one continuing borrowing with two reports offer a useful bounded
  product result, and is the boundary distinguishable from two loans on one
  home, refinancing, duplicate/corrected reports or uncertain association?
- Does P0 verify the actual tax meaning of reported interest, dated principal
  and the qualification/limit input, rather than silently choosing a sum,
  minimum or a convenient Box 2? Does it investigate what can support those
  inputs without asking the person to supply a tax verdict?
- Are report completeness, borrowing identity, loan-level circumstances,
  assumptions and unresolved treatment kept separate? Identify any missing
  consequential distinction now, but do not design every adjacent case.
- Will the proposed recorder/coordinator trace expose where the current
  second-report stop really occurs? Will it distinguish real offered-path
  evidence from supplied links or test-only declaration discovery?
- Does the plan look for adopted content/recording precedents before new
  schemas or engine work, keep single-report/saved-history compatibility,
  and examine correction/withdrawal and saved-result meaning?
- Are actions incremental with evidence reviewed before design selection?
  Reject both a premature implementation promise and an academic investigation
  with no named consumer. Missing implementation detail expected from P0 is
  not, by itself, a defect in the opening plan.

Return READY for P0 or specific material repairs, with the consequence and
smallest useful refinement. State any newly exposed owner scope choice in
plain language. No compulsory disagreements and no complete mortgage design.
