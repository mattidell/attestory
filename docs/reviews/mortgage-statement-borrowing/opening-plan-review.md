## Assessment

The goal is useful and bounded. A change in the payment collector gives an
ordinary reason for two reports without making two documents into two debts.
The plan's contrasting cases explicitly include two independent borrowings,
duplicates, corrected reports and inability to establish the relationship.
It does not treat a shared address, name or amount as identity evidence.

The consequential tax question occurs before representation selection.
“What makes this uncertain,” Action 2 and “Decisions and readiness” require
P0 to establish the needed balance/debt proposition and its support, rather
than select a minimum, sum, convenient Box 2 or annual-average interpretation.
Primary 2025 authority, reporting periods and acquisition versus origination
are named investigation subjects. The over-limit case is particularly useful:
it requires the principal to reach the comparison, addressing the prior
mortgage retrospective's concrete evidence failure. No deduction formula or
favorable outcome is promised by the opening plan.

The plan also separates report completeness from the account of all home debt.
Action 2 distinguishes supplied, assumed, derived and unresolved information;
the phase overview explains defaults separately from assumptions and from what
the person said. Actions 3 and 5 carry that distinction into questions,
authority, defaults and change handling. These obligations are sufficient for
P0; the exact question set and default policy are expected later outputs.

There is a named production consumer and a discriminating route investigation.
Action 2 traces recording, current support, marshalling, execution, Schedule A
and saved explanation, and requires reproducing the second-report stop through
the offered path. Action 4 explicitly denies that a supplied relationship or
test-only declaration proves production obtained it. Action 6 then demands
durable recovery and reloaded presentation. These commitments can expose a
recorder stop independently of transport, rule cardinality or display limits;
they do not assume that one discovered restriction explains the whole route.

Existing-first comparison is concrete without selecting a mechanism. The
adopted subtotal/subject-result pattern and explicit relationship recording
must be inspected before new schemas or coordinator work. The consolidation
retrospective explains why that investigation matters, but does not establish
that its mechanism solves borrowing-level tax meaning. The plan preserves
that evidence boundary. It also names single-report compatibility, saved
history, shared-account versus individual-form correction, withdrawn links,
late reports and removal/re-entry. Missing implementation-level lifecycle
details are not an opening defect because Actions 3–5 must settle them before
production.

The overview and roadmap consistently describe a bounded return after the
earlier endpoint was met. They neither retract earlier delivery nor count
this investigation as completed breadth. No new owner scope choice is exposed
by this review. If P0 finds the desired result requires broader debt allocation
or identity capability, the plan already requires a concrete scope/cost choice.
