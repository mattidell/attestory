# P0 — what the reports say, what the current path can record

Audience: Builder.

## Purpose

Establish what must be understood before choosing a same-borrowing design.
A person has one continuing home loan and two reports. Explain what can be
known from the documents, what the person could reasonably affirm, what the
tax rules need, and where the present application actually stops. Do not
design a full mortgage system or select a principal formula from convenience.

The independent opening-plan review is READY for investigation, not tax
correctness or implementation. Read its evidence ceiling.

## Two increments, one unit

### A — source account and proposed check, then return

1. Write a short plain-language account of the case, contrasting a change in
   who collects payments with a new borrowing, two independent loans on one
   home, and a corrected/duplicate report. Do not assume the person can tell
   these apart from an address or account string alone. List the material
   relationships and unknowns without designing their storage.
2. Verify the reporting meaning of Boxes 1, 2, 3 and 11 in the April 2025
   Form 1098 instructions. Read the relevant 2025 Publication 936 rules for
   qualifying debt, limits and balances, and primary statutory/regulatory
   provisions where the proposed deduction boundary depends on them. Record
   exact sections/pages and separate verified rules from unresolved application
   questions. Do not extend the April instructions' tax-summary language to
   later years or treat Box 2 as an annual average without authority.
3. Trace source to consumer: offered mortgage review/question, durable save
   and statement keys, current support, package/release resolution, input
   marshalling, rule cardinality and limit input, Schedule A and saved model.
   Name each independent one-report restriction, not just the first guard.
   Read the question next to what its consuming rule actually reads.
4. Name the exact baseline package/release. README's v45/v43 example and the
   later v49/v47 consolidation are not interchangeable evidence. Compare their
   mortgage selections and determine what must be exercised through which
   route. Do not relabel a historical fixture as latest-package execution or
   silently replace unrelated closure/adoption facts to obtain a pass.
5. Propose one bounded executable check, using existing synthetic recorder
   fixtures where possible. State exact entrypoints, cases, assertions,
   interpreter/commands, output location and cap before execution. Identify
   which data/relationships the fixture supplies, which answers the real
   recorder obtains, and what it cannot represent.

Return the first account in `p0-current-account.md` for Foreman feedback.
No execution probe, new pytest or production edit in increment A. This first
return is deliberately incomplete P0, not a design verdict. The Foreman will
commit the useful refinement and respond before increment B.

### B — execute only after the Foreman's continuation

Use real implementations and a fresh synthetic durable workspace. The initial
probe should cover these three observations, refining the commands from A:

| Case | What must be observed |
| --- | --- |
| One supported report | Actual review offers the selected question set; real durable answer save, recovery and coordinator reach the bounded amount/reason and reloaded presentation. Record actual package/release and pins. |
| Two distinct reports | Attempt offered review and direct durable ordinary-answer save for exact statement keys. Observe each real refusal and unchanged revision where a save refuses. Separately execute the actual coordinator to observe its own multiple-report disposition. Do not pretend either path records a same-loan relation that does not exist. |
| One deliberately over-limit principal | Show the actual Box 2 finding reaches the comparison, and record the real reason/disposition. A test with missing/zero principal or inferred status from an amount is not evidence. A false favorable result is a finding, not a new expected contract. |

If the chosen adopted package cannot reach the nominal baseline, report its
actual blocker and source. Preserve any successful historical-route evidence
at that scope. Do not fabricate a current route, weaken validation, mutate
finished output, or change production to make the probe run.

Read the adopted student-loan explicit-relationship and ordinary subtotal/
subject-result mechanisms and the sale transport precedent. State what each
already establishes and what it has not proved for a continuing mortgage.
Paper comparison is enough at this stage; new rival implementation needs a
separate consequential question and evidence ceiling.

Complete P0 with an integration-cost account: which restrictions are content,
recording, transport or consumer questions; which balance/debt proposition
remains to be supported; what can transfer; what needs a design choice; and
what would force scope back to the owner. Do not choose a new schema, loan
identifier, default policy or debt-limit method. No production promise follows
from successfully creating a synthetic log.

## Evidence and boundaries

- In each case distinguish supplied document values/scenario knowledge,
  recorded ordinary answers, application defaults, derived facts, unresolved
  facts and actual displayed statuses. A common-loan story supplied in a fixture
  is not evidence that the current application obtained that relationship.
- Keep report completeness distinct from all home-debt completeness. Inspect
  the exact adopted family claim rather than infer it from a family name.
- Map correction/withdrawal/late report consequences from actual sources and
  existing tests. New lifecycle execution is not required unless it answers a
  specific P0 blocker; mark unexecuted behavior honestly.
- Commit no source fixtures, goldens or regression tests in this unit. If a
  small reproducible probe is useful, keep it beside its evidence, clearly
  labeled diagnostic rather than production/test coverage. No full suite.
- A's proposed probe gets an elapsed cap of 300 seconds per command, configured
  parallelism for any proposed pytest, and no repeated pass-only confirmation.
  Use the configured dependency runtime; no dependency or project-config edit.
- Report material findings and their consequence, not every housekeeping
  observation. Independent Sol review of completed P0 precedes design selection.
