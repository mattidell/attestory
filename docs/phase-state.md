# Phase State

## Current result

**Personal-Item Sales: What Was Sold and What the Payment Means is closed.**
On a synthetic account, a person can record one Form 1099-K, or that none was
received, and answer ordinary questions about one sale of an item they bought
and used personally. They can correct or withdraw those answers. When the
account establishes a loss, the statement's Box 1a amount is entered at the
top of 2025 Schedule 1. Income and tax do not change.

A gain, break-even, "I can't tell", an excluded acquisition or use, an
unexplained Box 1a difference, additional seller shipping under platform
pass-through, a second statement and an unanswered answer are each named and
enter nothing. The saved explanation keeps the report, the answers, the
assumptions actually used (none), the published computation and the named
reason, without recomputing any amount.

The route is one statement and one sale. Gain reporting on Form 8949 and
Schedule D is separate future work. Accounts are synthetic only.
