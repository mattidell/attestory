## Narrow verdict

**Confirmed.** Repair 2 resolves Confirmation R1's sole decision-blocking
finding (measurement 3: the qualified-positive, both-absent case now names
both declaration symbols in one non-publication walk, and the single-absent
case names exactly the true absent set) without disturbing any of R1's five
passing measurements. D2-P1 and D2-P3 are verified genuinely unchanged by
direct comparison against `repair1/design.md`, not merely by the design's own
assertion. The one reported collateral finding — the package pin correction
from v1→v2 to v1→v3, required because `conditional_dependency_set` is
admissible only under `rule-artifact.v3` — is confirmed accurate against both
schema files and scoped only to D2-P2's successor-identity claim, with no
undisclosed change to D2-P1/D2-P3's committed shape. This verdict does not
reopen D1, D3, statutory-rate fidelity, or presentation, and does not extend
into evaluation analysis or ADR drafting.
