# Closure Freshness Process Retrospective

Date: 2026-07-12. Status: complete; ADR-0017 ratified.

## Outcome

Two paper rivals and one tiny reducer established a conforming late-member
currency mechanism: an ordinary family/scope horizon successor is an
individuation root; closure findings keyed on the predecessor horizon displace;
closure-backed results then displace through existing derivation edges.

The process rejected the incumbent's comparison-derived root and selected the
clean-room rival's recorded successor shape. It proved incremental/rebuild
equality, atomic rejection, no resurrection, and family isolation before any
production act/schema work.

## What worked

- The seven-case charter made the missing event/edge explicit.
- Clean-room rivalry distinguished “computed stale” from recorded succession.
- Governance and adversary reviews agreed on the precise reducer probe rather
  than demanding a production runner.
- The reducer exposed roots and both edge maps, making E7.2 reviewable.
- Review line caps stayed removed; reports were complete and useful.
- Proposition-specific depth worked: CF-P1 settled at paper while CF-P2 alone
  advanced to executable evidence.

## Defects and lessons

- Governance reviewers twice wrote to repository-root `reviews/`; explicit full
  output paths fixed the next round.
- Prototype input validation accepted `None` horizon and empty IDs; this did not
  alter the edge decision but must become production schema negatives.
- Public mutable reducer state allowed out-of-contract tamper; production
  projection must be encapsulated or rebuilt only from immutable accepted acts.
- Value-correction replay lacked unique act identity; production act registry
  and idempotence must cover it.
- “Ordinary citizen” is not enough: the production plan must name actor,
  atomic transition, revision/order, predecessor, family declaration/version,
  scope, and admission rejection.

## Adoption boundary

Prototype code is evidence only. Production must instantiate ADR-0017 through
schema-first acts/citizens, registered atomic admission, ordinary entity
supersession, existing currency edges, rebuild equality, and strict malformed-
transition negatives. Governance text is unchanged; ADR-0017 extends existing
individuation use rather than adding an edge.
