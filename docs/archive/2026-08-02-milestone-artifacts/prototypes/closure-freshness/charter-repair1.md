## Question

horizon successor, cause closure fact/finding displacement by individuation,
and cause zero displacement by existing derivation edges—rebuildably, without a
third edge, derived authority, listener, staleness bit, or manual withdrawal?

1. Contract note declaring horizon kind, `(family declaration, scope)` identity,

2. Tiny reducer over ordered synthetic acts. After every accepted act, compare

4. Negatives: missing successor; fabricated future/replayed/mis-scoped horizon;
   half transition; global horizon; derived closure; caller staleness flag.
5. At least two test-local mutants: omit horizon successor and directly inject a
   stale root/flag. The suite must kill both.
