Medium tier. Foreman-spawned sub-agent (standing authorization: owner-approved
plan, Gate 8; ADR-0013 reviewer-dispatch amendment).

Review both iteration designs (`it1/design.md`, `it2/design.md`) and their
examinations for conformance with the governance constitution/ontology and the
kernel/derivation contracts (append-only act log, no mock or stub findings in

rival design. Classify every finding; do not repair designs.

Output: `reviews/round-2-governance.md`. Within-round independence: do not read
the adversary's round-2 review.
