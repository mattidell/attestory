# Intake Principles

**The Principles**

*The generative layer. These bind nothing; they interpret first where the Constitution and Ontology are silent. Each principle is the conviction from which its articles descend, stated with its argument. Articles cite principles as parentage; a principle generating no articles is decorative; an article descending from no principle is unjustified. Both are defects.*

---

**I. Facts are peers, not document children.** *(→ Art. 1)*
A finding is authoritative because someone determined it, not because a paper contains it. Documents are evidence for determinations, never containers of them. A question about your situation exists regardless of which page mentions it; tie its identity to a file and the question vanishes or doubles as documents come and go. Every finding stands on its own act; no document owns one.

**II. The system proposes; the user signs.** *(→ Art. 2)*
Nothing the machinery produces has standing until a person, shown it exactly, takes it. This is not caution about model quality; it is the design. Nondeterministic processes propose, the user disposes, deterministic machinery elaborates — each side has the properties its role requires, and neither could do the other's job. A signature over something unseen is not a signature.

**III. Judgment lives with the facts.** *(→ Art. 3)*
Where the law leaves a question open, only a person closes it, and the closing is a visible, attributable act sitting among the facts it governs. There is no separate place decisions live, and no computation that quietly makes one. Every open path is closed by an act.

**IV. Authority is compositional.** *(→ Art. 4, 12)*
The user vouches two ways: findings one at a time, machinery wholesale. A derived result contains nothing but vouched inputs transformed by vouched rules, so it needs no third vouching — demanding one would be theater. Adoption is bounded rather than blank because everything adopted can be read and everything adopted is pinned. What you did not sign, you adopted; nothing else runs.

**V. The workspace is the record.** *(→ Art. 5)*
One place holds everything authoritative; everything else is a rendering. The moment a second store becomes necessary, the record has become a negotiation among participants. Destroy anything but the workspace and nothing is lost — that sentence is the architecture.

**VI. Never wrong, only incomplete.** *(→ Art. 6, 8)*
The workspace may be missing anything; it may misstate nothing, and it may hide nothing. Stop any process anywhere, step off any path: what you see is true and everything is there. Incompleteness is a fact honestly shown; partiality-as-truth and truth-behind-doors are the two ways a record lies.

**VII. History only accumulates.** *(→ Art. 7)*
Nothing is edited; effects are displaced by later acts and the past stays put. Currency is a reading of the record, not a property maintained beside it. One mechanism, rule-governed per kind — a wage figure corrects freely, a method choice waits on the rules that govern method changes — because how freely an answer may change is itself part of what the question is.

**VIII. Meaning is declared or it does not exist.** *(→ Art. 9, 10)*
Every thing names the schema that defines it, and the schema — not the reader — says what it is. Private understandings are how meaning escapes the record: the tolerant parser, the repurposed field, the payload three components "understand." The gate stands at the door: if it's in the workspace, its schema was there first.

**IX. Obtuse is permitted; hidden is not.** *(→ Art. 11)*
All tax meaning lives in declared, versioned, inspectable artifacts; the engine is thin and any conforming runner yields the same findings. Rules may be complex beyond any casual reader — the contract permits obtuse. What it forbids is meaning discoverable only by reading code or probing behavior, because adoption of machinery is meaningful exactly insofar as the machinery could be read.

**X. Computation publishes; it never edits.** *(→ Art. 13)*
Derivation touches the workspace one way: complete findings, published against declared eligibility, to saturation. No form state mutated in place, no totals kept in lockstep, no orchestrated march through a return. Delete every derived finding; rerun; the same findings return — reproducibility is what lets published results carry authority no one signed per-output.

**XI. Every execution answers for itself.** *(→ Art. 14)*
Whatever reads or writes the record leaves a record: what it saw, under which versions, what it made, what stopped it. Current state cannot testify about past process — nothing in today's findings remembers what yesterday's run couldn't reach. Failures record too; a run that published nothing still happened.

**XII. Every value carries its explanation.** *(→ Art. 15)*
From any number, walk to the findings, rules, acts, and runs beneath it — grounded in the record, never reconstructed. A plausible story about where a value probably came from is not an explanation. A value without one is invalid state, however correct it looks.

**XIII. Holding is not license.** *(→ Art. 16, 17)*
The workspace's totality is an architectural property, not an invitation. What the machinery may consult is the user's grant; what is operative for a task is the user's context; both are visible, specific, and on the record. What read your data, and why, was always yours to allow.

**XIV. The quarantine is a place.** *(→ Art. 18)*
Personal data lives where structure confines it, and structure — not trust — is the boundary. Everything outside could be published; everything inside serves only its owner. Sensitivity travels by description, so the records of a life are as protected as the life. And nothing outside needs what's inside: synthetic by default is what makes the wall livable.

**XV. Legal weight enters once.** *(→ Art. 19)*
Everything upstream of filing is the user's working record — operative for the workspace, weightless to the law. Filing is where the user, not the system, performs the legal act over a fixed representation, and where the weight deliberately changes. The system computes; the user files. A tool that blurred that line would be claiming an authority it must never hold.
