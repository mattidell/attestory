# Claim Boundary Exploration — Actionable Considerations

Audience: Product (selection instrument); Shared (status)

Status: **active selection instrument.** This is a non-authoritative
exploration register, not a backlog, product contract, ontology, or promise to
pursue every entry.

## Admission rule

A consideration belongs here only when it identifies:

1. a concrete user question, misleading implication, system limitation, or
   bounded research question; and
2. at least one plausible action the project could later take because of what
   it learns.

An entry may remain broad, disputed, or unselected. It may not remain merely
interesting. If its action path disappears, remove it during consolidation or
return it to temporary research notes.

## Consolidation rule

At each milestone boundary:

- merge duplicate considerations around the product question they affect;
- split an entry when its plausible actions require different evidence;
- remove an entry whose only remaining value is general intellectual interest;
- preserve unresolved disagreement when a later decision could turn on it; and
- promote nothing automatically into implementation, governance, or a future
  milestone.
