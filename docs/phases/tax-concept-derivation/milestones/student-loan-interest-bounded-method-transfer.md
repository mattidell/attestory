# Student Loan Interest Bounded Method Transfer

## Milestone identity

- Phase: Tax Concept Derivation
- Milestone key: `student-loan-interest-bounded-method-transfer`
- Primary branch: `milestone/student-loan-interest-bounded-method-transfer`
- State: **CLOSED 2026-09-15** — validated method, production deferred (result 3)
- Roadmap role: resume the student-loan contrast while leaving Identified
  Evaluation Context as an unselected backlog investigation

## Plain-language purpose

Nominee interest established a useful product method: preserve what a document
reported, let a person describe an ordinary circumstance, retain who said what,
have an adopted tax rule derive the tax consequence, and carry that consequence
to the return with recoverable provenance.

The preceding student-loan milestone was chosen to test whether that method
transfers beyond an ownership allocation. It found valuable additional tax
concepts, selected eligible-student status as a forcing case, and then stopped
when its prototype could not evaluate every Form 1098-E statement through the
existing pairing machinery. That showed a limitation of the attempted
decomposition. It did not establish that ordinary product use must independently
reconstruct every constituent of eligible-student or qualified-loan status.

This milestone resumes the original question:

> Can the product use the nominee-interest method for one bounded student-loan
> circumstance while keeping the document, the person's ordinary statement,
> the intermediate tax concept, and the deduction consequence distinct?

The milestone treats eligible-student status as a real tax concept. It compares
ways that concept may receive support instead of assuming one universal route:

1. practical reliance on an uncontradicted information return for the bounded
   classification it represents;
2. focused reevaluation when an ordinary fact contradicts that classification;
   and
3. explicit derivation from constituent facts where a concrete product case
   actually requires it.

The first two may be enough to validate the translation method. The third may
eventually need the proposed Identified Evaluation Context capability, but that
capability is not a prerequisite or a predetermined outcome here.

## What carries forward

The prior milestone's completed work is retained rather than repeated:

- its plain-language map of the deduction and thirteen existing answers;
- its ten-constituent tax-boundary analysis;
- its artifact and consumer map, revalidated where code behavior is relied on;
- its fixed distinction between a person's statement and a rule-owned tax
  conclusion;
- its current worksheet, cap, phaseout, Schedule 1, AGI, lifecycle, and
  provenance observations; and
- its feasibility probe as evidence that the attempted association-driven
  per-statement decomposition can silently omit an unassociated statement.

That probe remains evidence about that mechanism. It is not evidence that the
product must perform the decomposition, that Form 1098-E proves every legal
predicate, or that a general context schema is required.

## Selected boundary

### The product method under test

The reusable candidate is a sequence of responsibility, not a shared schema:

1. preserve documentary evidence and what it reports;
2. record an ordinary statement at the scope the person can honestly address;
3. keep the statement separate from the tax concept it may support or
   contradict;
4. let an adopted rule own the classification and numerical consequence;
5. preserve correction, retraction, invalidation, and provenance; and
6. project the result to the return without describing the method as complete
   support for the entire tax domain.

The nominee implementation is accepted comparison evidence. It is not a
migration target. No work in this milestone supersedes its report-group
coordinator, contracts, facts, rules, package, or presentation.

### The bounded student-loan experiment

P0 selects one ordinary circumstance from the prior eligible-student analysis
that a person can state without supplying the legal conclusion. The preferred
discriminator is a clear fact about enrollment in a degree, certificate, or
other credential program during an identified academic period. P0
must verify the controlling tax operation and may choose a different
eligible-student constituent if this fact cannot produce an honest bounded
positive and negative pair.

**Selected and confirmed at P0.** That discriminator is kept. Two boundaries on
it, established by the P0 charter:

- The fact is about the **student at an identified academic period**, with a
  program relationship. It is not a property of the indebtedness. The loan rule
  applies it to an identified loan through the relation between loan proceeds,
  education, and academic period — and that relation is separate support the
  route must have, not a property of the ordinary fact.
- The user is **never** asked whether a credential is legally "recognized."
  Credential recognition, institution eligibility, and the institution-certified
  half-time threshold are determinations the rule owns from institutional or
  public authority, and none has an evidence route at this base.

The adverse answer avoids all three of those authority determinations; the
favorable answer needs all three. That asymmetry is the point of the experiment,
not a defect in it.

The fixture may hold other qualification conditions constant as **experimental
premises**. Each premise must be named. A premise can support prototype
discrimination; it cannot support a production tax claim. Production is
available only if P2 and Track 0 establish a real evidence route for every
load-bearing premise used by the selected product result.

The narrow starting fixture is one tax-return filer, one synthetic Form 1098-E,
one identified loan, one identified academic period, and education for that
tax-return filer.
Multi-statement and multi-loan cases test the boundary. Until their identities
and attribution can be handled honestly, the bounded route must not invent
uniformity — but P0 does not preselect refusal as the answer. Three evidence
states govern (P0 charter § 5.3): no relevant ordinary evidence, where the
**incumbent calculation** is measured and every support it actually uses is named;
relevant evidence that can be attributed honestly; and relevant evidence that
cannot be attributed, where the dependent deduction must **block or refuse rather
than fall back silently** to the prior aggregate result. Absence of the ordinary fact is never read as favorable.

**Refusal is not support, and refusal is not free.** Refusing the multi-loan and
multi-period classes disposes of the classes the route does not cover; it
supplies no evidence for the single-loan single-period class the route does
cover. And P0 established that **no cardinality refusal exists for the Form
1098-E family today**: the current route deliberately *aggregates* multiple
statements, and Track 6b repaired it specifically to keep doing so. Refusing a
multi-statement return is therefore a **narrowing of currently supported
behaviour**, not a reuse of existing machinery, and must be justified as such. The accountable
statement-to-loan-and-period relationship that class depends on has no committed
production path at this base, so it is a production obligation in its own right
— to be given an honest route or carried explicitly into the P2 decision, never
laundered into a contract.

### Three support routes remain distinct

| Route | What it means | What it can establish here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document-reliance (**proposed, not adopted; not implemented in production**) | The application would rely operationally on an uncontradicted Form 1098-E classification within a declared bounded use | A possible practical preparation path, not independent proof of every constituent predicate. Form 1098-E alone is insufficient for § 221(d)(1)(C) **and does not by itself satisfy the current nonempty worksheet dependencies**; whether it supplies bounded positive support depends on additional properly scoped evidence of the reporting basis for the relevant loan or amount, and on a declared reliance policy |
| Qualification-rebutting (adverse ordinary evidence) | An ordinary fact defeats a separate § 221 condition while the documentary report remains accurate, and the tax rule evaluates the consequence | Whether the nominee method transfers to a rule-owned intermediate tax concept |
| Constituent-derived | The application obtains enough independently scoped facts to evaluate the legal definition directly | Only what the actual supplied facts and authorities support; not presumed necessary or available |

Absence of contradictory information is not itself a user assertion. A
document-reliance route must not fabricate an affirmation that the person
personally verified every upstream condition. Nor is the incumbent route a
document-only path: it depends on the Form 1098-E-derived subtotal, 24 direct
determinable inputs, the derived total-income finding, family closure, and three
parameters (P0 charter § 3.0).

### Evaluation Context stays deferred

The proposed Identified Evaluation Context is a possible execution facility for
rules that genuinely must account separately for every identified subject and
every missing or ambiguous relation. It is not an intrinsic field of a tax
claim and not a container for every relevant legal or evidentiary condition.

This milestone records a reopening trigger instead of designing it:

> Reopen Identified Evaluation Context when an adopted product route requires
> per-subject constituent evaluation, cannot rely honestly on a document or a
> bounded specialized mechanism, and has an observable error if one subject is
> omitted.

**P0 outcome: the trigger is not met, and not ruled out. No single case decides
it.** The single-subject cases (M0-M5, M8, M9) need no per-subject evaluation.
**M6 and M7 both** bear on the evaluation subject, because the IRS permits one
Form 1098-E per loan *or* one for the interest from all of a borrower's loans, and
the form does not say which was filed — so statement-level evaluation does not
solve loan-level attribution.

The trigger has three conjuncts, and establishing only some of them does not meet
it. Per-subject execution would establish a need for **separately attributable
evaluation**, not for a general Evaluation Context: the conjunct requiring that
neither a document nor a **bounded specialized mechanism** can carry it is
**untested**.

So P1 must observe and report two things — whether uniform/unkeyed handling
produces an **observable error**, and whether a **bounded relationship or
specialized mechanism** can handle the identified subjects — together with
candidate costs. **P2 or Track 0 recommends the product boundary; a run cannot
choose that product policy by itself.** See the P0 charter §§ 5.0, 5.0a, 5.3.

## Questions to answer

1. What proposition does Form 1098-E itself represent, and how far may the
   application rely on it for ordinary preparation when nothing conflicts?
2. Which eligible-student constituent can a person describe in ordinary
   language without being asked for the legal conclusion?
3. What explicit premises are required to make the experiment bounded, and
   which of them prevent production adoption?
4. Can the existing assertion, retraction, rule, worksheet, run, and provenance
   boundaries carry the selected circumstance without a new per-subject
   coordinator?
5. When an ordinary fact defeats a § 221 condition, does the application derive
   an adverse or unresolved tax result while leaving the reported document fact
   accurate and unrewritten?
6. What is actually reused from nominee interest: conceptual separation,
   existing contracts, implementation machinery, or only the development
   method?
7. Does any observed behavior genuinely require the deferred Evaluation Context
   capability — as distinct from requiring separately attributable evaluation that
   a bounded relationship or specialized mechanism could carry?

   product error meets **every** conjunct of its reopening trigger — including
   that no bounded specialized mechanism can carry the identified subjects; and
9. the result leaves a short plain-language account of what the experiment
   taught the product about translating documents and ordinary life into tax
   concepts.

The milestone may close successfully without production code if it validates
the method but cannot yet supply an honest evidence route for a material
premise. It must not describe that outcome as complete student-loan support.

## Stop conditions

Stop and return to the owner if:

- the selected ordinary question asks the user to provide a tax conclusion;
- an experimental premise would have to become implicit production authority;
- honest handling requires a general Evaluation Context or institutional
  authority mechanism rather than the bounded experiment;
- a multi-statement or multi-loan case can be handled honestly neither by
  attribution, nor by a bounded specialized mechanism, nor by blocking or
  refusing, without redesigning current identity generally;
- the selected consequence would require superseding nominee interest; or
- the work expands into complete student-loan, education, household, or user-
  explanation modeling.
