# Track 13 — experimental explicit relationship recording contract

The owner approved the bounded recorder recommended after Track 12 on
2026-09-29. This is a synthetic experiment over the existing Track 9–11
declarations. It is not a production answer format, reference namespace,
tax-policy selection, or published schema.

## Claims the experiment may record

An account may retain references and knownness without asserting a relationship.
Recording a relationship requires a separately identified, affirmative supplied
claim with its own evidence and contribution authority:

1. **Financing:** this identified borrowing financed this identified schooling
   circumstance. The experiment uses the existing `FINANCE` fact type, whose
   identity is the borrowing key plus all four identity keys of the current
   schooling source fact.
2. **Statement membership:** this identified borrowing is associated with this
   identified statement. Its stated scope is that one named statement record,
   represented by the existing `MEMBERSHIP` keys: lender, statement, tax year,
   and borrowing. It says nothing about the fraction of interest, whole-loan
   treatment, legal indebtedness, or deduction.

The two claims have separate stable claim addresses. An answer may supply one,
both, or neither. A missing, unknown, negative, or unconfirmed connection does
not become an affirmative claim. Reference co-presence, equal values, account
knownness, a statement amount, and result names never assert a relationship.

## Exact synthetic target convention

Only within this experiment, an explicit borrowing reference denotes a current
borrowing entity ID; a schooling reference denotes one current exact `SCHOOL`
source fact ID; and a statement reference denotes one current exact source fact
ID for the named statement's box-1 fact. The recorder verifies the target kind,
standing and structured keys in the fresh projection. It copies no key from a
rendered symbol and never guesses a referent from amount, name, order or equal
value. Missing, stale, wrong-kind and duplicate active relationship identities
refuse recording without manufacturing a source finding. This fixture convention
does not bind future product identifiers.

## Authority and lifecycle

Every recorded relationship finding identifies the explicit supplied claim's
evidence and contribution. The existing kernel admission and persisted ActLog
remain the source of authority; a test may not inject a derived result or bind
the runner directly. The consumer's actual pins must reach the recorded
relationship findings and their independent schooling/statement sources where
read. The account is context unless a rule actually reads it; no synthetic
account pin is added to a relationship result.

An account-only correction or retraction changes only the account. A correction
or withdrawal naming one explicit relationship claim changes only that claim's
current support. If a correction changes the relationship identity, the prior
finding is explicitly retired and the successor separately admitted; historical
findings are not retargeted. A school or statement source correction does not
silently rewrite the supplied relationship claim. Its downstream consequences
must follow only the dependency actually declared and pinned.

This experiment may reuse the accepted Track 11 expected/available declaration
for a neutral consumer. The application treatment stays `unadopted`. No
favorable tax result, default from an empty collection, worksheet deduction,
reader integration, or ADR 0076 Part 3 selection follows from the recorder.

## Acceptance boundary

The experiment succeeds when explicit claims pass real contribution and package
admission, survive fresh ActLog recovery, and reach the existing relationship
consumers with exact subject identities, categorical types and complete source
sets in both runners and live coordination. Saved evidence reopens after the
disposable workspace and live result objects are gone. Negative and lifecycle
cases retain exact refusal, standing and lineage. If the current machinery
cannot preserve this narrow contract, retain the smallest admitted failing
case and name the missing capability; do not substitute a hand-built result.
