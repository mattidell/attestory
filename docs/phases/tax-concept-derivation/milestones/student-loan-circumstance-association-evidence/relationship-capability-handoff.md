# Relationship capability — bounded handoff

**We established how ordinary relationship statements can become durable,
independently changeable information that calculations can use and explain.**
This is an approved explicit partial result. G2 and G3 remain unpassed.

## Established capability

A person can identify the borrowing and schooling circumstance they mean and
affirm that the borrowing financed that schooling. Separately, they can affirm
that an identified interest statement includes interest associated with the
borrowing. One reviewed action can contain both answers without merging their
meanings. Each has its own evidence and can be corrected or withdrawn
independently. If the person cannot distinguish the intended things, the
application preserves uncertainty rather than selecting for them. An unknown
interest portion does not undo an otherwise known relationship.

The saved record preserves answers, recognition context, current and historical
findings, and their support. The reviewed statement-correction route
distinguishes a known amount-only change, a named inclusion addition or
removal, and uncertainty about a named pair. `Cannot tell` retains the earlier
affirmation in history but ends its usable support; later `yes` or `no` changes
that pair's current standing. A separate pair and statement remain independent.
An unidentified replacement statement is refused rather than matched by
label, amount, order or opaque address.

Fresh ActLog recovery and both admitted runners demonstrate a **neutral**
consumer of the current relationship, current source and exact answer, plus a
separate unresolved observation. The consumer pins the supporting findings;
it does not calculate or verify a tax conclusion. The
[Track 18 report](track18-relationship-correction-report.md) records the
executed cases, interrupted-write limits and verification.

## Remaining integration and bounded tax work

**Implementation work:** a production statement-update entry path must use
the scope-bearing correction workflow or enforce its equivalent. A direct
source write bypasses that scope today and can leave an old inclusion usable.
The bounded multi-act recorder also needs an appropriate production recovery
or continuation design; its tested interrupted transitions are fail closed,
not atomic. Later tax work must connect supported relationships to the
specific tax conclusions and amounts it undertakes, preserve their sources and
refusals through saved recovery, and integrate any selected result with the
actual worksheet. G2's partial statement reduction, keyed categorical
conclusion and saved meaning, and G3's refusal cases and handoff remain
unexecuted. None requires modelling every possible schooling circumstance
before a bounded supported route can be built.

**Open product decisions:** ADR 0076 Part 3 has not selected how mixed-period
indebtedness affects a tax result. The scope of later automated treatment,
including which specialized adjustments the application will compute, also
requires a deliberate decision. The experimental reader's final wording,
layout and integration remain unaccepted. No choice here changes the existing
default for genuine silence or turns an unresolved link into a negative fact.

## Deliberately external or person-supplied possibilities

Calculating how much interest belongs to a borrowing is one task. Deciding
whether that allocation is the appropriate tax treatment is another.
Recording and using a result supplied by a person is a third. The product
does not promise to automate all three for mixed periods, allocations or
specialized adjustments. Where automated support ends, it may ask the person
for a scoped amount or determination. It should explain the particular
information or determination needed without requiring them to understand the
whole tax domain.

Any later person-supplied value must retain what statement, borrowing, period
or tax concept it applies to and who supplied it, so the application can
explain its use. It must remain distinguishable from an engine-derived
conclusion and must not silently become an engine-verified tax determination.
Canonical tax concepts still matter: their values can come from different
supported sources. This paragraph states product intent for a later design;
it does not authorize a manual-input mechanism in this milestone.

The original tax vertical is incomplete. The worksheet deduction remains
separate, and this neutral relationship result passes neither G2 nor G3.
