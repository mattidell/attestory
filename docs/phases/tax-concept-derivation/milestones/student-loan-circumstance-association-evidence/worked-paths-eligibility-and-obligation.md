# Two worked paths — eligibility, and legal obligation

Owner direction, 2026-09-22. Both tax-concept facts start from a **favourable default**.
They differ only in whether the translation layer can reach the value through enumerated
disqualifiers or has to ask more directly.

Each path shows the input, the translation, the resulting tax-concept value, **its
basis**, and its consumer. The basis is the point: three different bases can produce the
same value and must never be confused.

## Path 1 — eligible-student status

Tax-concept fact, held in the rule's shape: *the student was an eligible student for the
period the borrowing financed* (§ 221(d)(1)(C)).

| | A — nothing said | B — adverse circumstance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Input** | A Form 1098-E is entered. Nothing is said about schooling | "That term I was taking individual classes, not enrolled in any degree or certificate programme" |
| **Translation** | Favourable default; no enumerated disqualifier present | A supported, enumerated disqualifying circumstance is present and defeats the default |
| **Tax-concept value** | eligible student: **favourable** | eligible student: **not satisfied** |
| **Basis** | **default-supported** — neither proven nor attested by anyone | **derived from a stated circumstance** |
| **Consumer** | § 221(d)(1)(C), through the qualified-education-loan constituent, into the deduction | Same consumer, opposite outcome |

**What the favourable value is not.** It is not independent proof of every constituent,
and it is not a user affirmation — nobody said they were an eligible student. It is a
value the application derived from a default it selected, and its basis says so.

**What stays a responsibility regardless.** Institutional eligibility and credential
recognition remain conditions of the rule, represented and inspectable. Producing the
favourable value does **not** manufacture findings establishing them, and the
responsibility message is what tells the person they are theirs.

**A caution the previous review earned.** A broad ordinary answer is not automatically a
statutory disqualifier. "I attended evening classes" defeats nothing — evening
attendance is compatible with full-time credential enrolment. Only *supported,
enumerated* disqualifying circumstances change the default.

## Path 2 — legal obligation

Tax-concept fact, in the rule's shape: *the taxpayer has a legal obligation to make
interest payments* (26 CFR § 1.221-1(b)(1)).

| | A — asked and answered | B — not answered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Input** | "Yes, I'm the one paying it" | The question rests unanswered |
| **Translation** | Direct answer accepted, because the circumstances that would defeat an obligation **have not been decomposed** — there are no enumerated disqualifiers to ask for instead | Favourable default holds |
| **Tax-concept value** | legally obligated: **favourable** | legally obligated: **favourable** |
| **Basis** | **direct user answer** | **default-supported** |
| **Consumer** | § 1.221-1(b)(1)'s entitlement condition, into the deduction | Same |

**Same value, two bases, and they must stay distinguishable.** A person who said "yes"
and a person who said nothing produce the same tax-concept value and are not in the same
epistemic position. A later action that treats them alike — in explanation, in
correction, or in what it asks next — has lost something real.

**Why this path is shaped differently.** Not because a legal obligation is a different
*kind* of condition, but because its defeating circumstances are not yet decomposed. If
they are later enumerated, this path becomes path 1's shape: assume the default, ask for
departures. That is a translation-layer change and it would not touch the fact.

## What neither path decides

**How many canonical facts, or how many derivation steps.** Both paths are written as
input → value because that is what makes the basis visible, not because one fact and one
step is the design. Tax-concept facts, ordinary-circumstance representations and
intermediate derivations are all available, and **A5 decides how they connect.** Nothing
here prohibits any of those layers.

**Two further cautions carried forward.** An existing classification witness is not
necessarily the underlying circumstance — the incumbent per-statement witnesses sit at
the classification level, and the ordinary circumstances beneath them are different
facts. And the incumbent 529 witness does not define the full § 221(e)(1) rule; it is one
consumer's reading of part of it.
