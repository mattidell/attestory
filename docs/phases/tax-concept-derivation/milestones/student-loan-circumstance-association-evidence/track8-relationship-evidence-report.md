The current demo fact identity requires the borrowing key, so this specific representation cannot preserve a present membership claim whose borrowing identity is unresolved. The no-link control remains a valid, different state. This is a limitation of the exercised citizen/input shape, not a claim that all engine machinery is unable to model unresolved identities.

In the linked synthetic case, the saved model records a linked route and the experimental reader's other axes. Its statement-only bare conclusion is `inapplicable`, and responsibility rows report their declared missing conclusion dependency. That case does not test a missing schooling input or an unadopted mixed-period rule separately. The package executes no borrowing-level tax result, and the Track 8 experiment does not fabricate an `unresolved` tax result or reuse a missing-fact diagnostic to stand for a rule boundary.

The Track 7 owner direction explicitly leaves the mixed-period consequence unselected. Consequently this work does not label the prototype's `0` per-link values, `linked` route, or unchanged statement amount as a tax consequence.
