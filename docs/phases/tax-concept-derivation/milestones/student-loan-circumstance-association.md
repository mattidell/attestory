# Student Loan Circumstance Association

Milestone key: `student-loan-circumstance-association`.
Primary branch: `milestone/student-loan-circumstance-association`.
Result: **approved explicit partial result**. The original tax vertical is not
complete. No next milestone is selected.

## Accomplishment and boundary

**We established how ordinary relationship statements can become durable,
independently changeable information that calculations can use and explain.**
A person can identify a borrowing and schooling circumstance, say that the
borrowing financed that schooling, and separately say that an identified
interest statement includes interest on that borrowing. These are different
claims with separate evidence and standing. Reviewed actions can save both,
correct or withdraw either, and preserve uncertainty when a connection cannot
be established. Fresh recovery retains current support and historical answers.

The [capability handoff](student-loan-circumstance-association-evidence/relationship-capability-handoff.md)
states what a later consumer may safely use and what remains open. The executed
consumer is **neutral**: it observes supported relationships and unresolved
status; it does not produce an eligible amount, a tax conclusion, or a worksheet
deduction. A correction made outside the reviewed scope-bearing route can
still bypass that route's applicability guarantee.

shared-payment case comes from their ordinary account or remains unresolved.

## Executed result

- The engine now carries keyed publications, declared per-subject scheduling,
  link-coverage behavior and exact categorical type authority through package
  admission, marshalling and both runners. The corresponding production code,
  published successor schemas and focused regression tests are the reproducible
  record. Published schema history was extended by new versions, not revised.
- The bounded reader loads its explanation from a saved presentation after the
  live run is gone. It distinguishes what was supplied, what the calculation
  assumed, what it concluded and what remains the person's responsibility;
  technical evidence remains inspectable. This is experimental reader evidence,
  not worksheet integration or final text acceptance.
- Relationship recording, review, revision-bound save and correction operate
  through an ActLog. The v34–v36 synthetic release sequence admits neutral
  consumers that identify exact relationship and target source support. The
  [Track 18 report](student-loan-circumstance-association-evidence/track18-relationship-correction-report.md)
  exercises amount-only correction, named inclusion changes, uncertainty,
  later answers, saved recovery, full relevant pins and independent statements
  through both runners. Tested interrupted answer writes end obsolete usable
  support before reporting an answer saved; the multi-act operation is not
  atomic.

The final synthetic fixtures and tests retain positive and negative cases.
The Track 18 captured local full suite passed **2,420 tests with 20 skipped**;
its log and executable hashes are recorded in the report. The final PR's
`verify` check, when separately authorized for publication, remains the gate of

## Gate disposition and carried work

| Gate | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| G1 | Passed on the fact, question, applicability and bounded-engine evidence. |
| G2a | Passed for the bounded mechanism track. |
| G2 | **Unpassed.** The selected representation has not produced the partial statement reduction, keyed categorical tax conclusion and saved meaning under an adopted tax treatment. |
| G3 | **Unpassed.** The later tax producer's refusal cases and final tax handoff have not run. The owner approved closing this milestone as a partial result rather than treating those obligations as fulfilled. |

The carried work has three different standings:

1. **Implementation:** a production statement-update path must enforce the
   reviewed correction scope or its equivalent. Later bounded tax work must
   connect supported relationships to whatever conclusions and amounts it
   undertakes, retain exact provenance and refusals through recovery, and
   integrate a selected result with the actual worksheet. Production use of a
   multi-act recorder needs an appropriate interrupted-prefix recovery or
   continuation design. No requirement says every possible circumstance must
   be modeled before a bounded supported route can be built.
2. **Open product decisions:** ADR 0076 Part 3 has not decided mixed-period
   indebtedness treatment. The extent of automated interest allocation and
   specialized adjustments, and final reader wording and integration, remain
   to be selected for their own product scope.
3. **Deliberately external or person-supplied possibilities:** calculating an
   allocation, deciding whether it is an appropriate tax treatment, and
   recording or using a supplied result are separate tasks. A later product
   may ask for a scoped amount or determination when specialized work is
   outside automated support. It should explain the specific information
   needed without requiring the person to understand the whole tax domain.
   Any supplied value must keep its scope and provenance, remain distinct from
   an engine-derived conclusion, and never silently become an engine-verified
   tax determination. Canonical tax concepts remain useful even when values
   come from different supported sources. No manual-input mechanism is
   authorized or built by this milestone.

The [handoff](student-loan-circumstance-association-evidence/relationship-capability-handoff.md)
is the continuing boundary. The actual worksheet, favorable eligibility,
interest allocation, and tax producer are not claims of this result. The
Engine Breadth Form 1098-E coverage frontier is unchanged: its earlier
synthetic-complete worksheet class is separate from this relationship work.
