# Shared Understanding in Development

## Purpose

Investigate what makes a working model useful for communication and judgment
between agents and between agents and the owner. The purpose is experimentation:
construct hypotheses, run trials, gather observations, evaluate explanations,
and choose what to investigate next.

A model can be a few sentences, a sketch or a shorthand relationship. It can
help someone begin an investigation or communicate an understanding already
developed elsewhere. Its usefulness is not established by producing, polishing
or approving the document. This phase asks what someone can notice and do with
it, what it conceals, and how they revise it when evidence changes their basis.

The owner has explicitly declined to carry forward the products of the previous
Planning with a Case Expert run. That unmerged branch is preserved as reference
for setup, observations and experimental limitations. Its proposed domain model
and working-account practice are not adopted by this phase plan.

## Actions the team will take

1. Frame a specific question and construct a hypothesis about how a model could
   help an acting agent understand a consequential situation.
2. Select a bounded task and design observations that could support, weaken or
   leave the hypothesis unsettled.
3. Prepare experimental material and review the design before exposing it to
   participants.
4. Conduct the trial and preserve the participants' explicit working accounts,
   inquiries, decisions and revisions as data.
5. Evaluate the observations against the hypothesis, including competing
   explanations and the influence of the experimental setup.
6. Report what was learned and propose the next discriminating experiment.

The foreman facilitates these actions; it is not the experimental participant.
The roadmap changes with the evidence and owner feedback.

## What we want to understand

Which characteristics of a model help an agent recognize the stakes, identify
missing grounds, and choose an investigation? When does a fluent description
become a plausible stand-in for the thing being reasoned about? Does expressing
a working understanding make a misunderstanding visible enough for the acting
agent to diagnose it and change its own course?

Subject expertise, access to evidence, model presentation, prompting and expert
intervention can each explain an apparent improvement. Experiments should
distinguish these where possible and disclose them where they cannot. A
successful result or agreeable conversation is not by itself a test of a
hypothesis about modeling.

## Material and stakes

Existing student-loan work supplies consequential situations without requiring
another tax implementation. In particular, statements may concern an identified
borrowing or schooling circumstance, while a calculation relies on their
normalized meaning. Losing the relation between an answer and what it concerns
can change what the application claims to know.

The subject's provisional model and plan are experimental outputs. They may
be incomplete, wrong or useful; none becomes a product contract or development
commitment merely because it is produced. Product changes belong to separately
selected work. Experimental information is synthetic and source claims are
checked against the relevant committed evidence.

## Results and phase boundary

The durable results are experiment designs, material evidence, evaluations of
the hypotheses and the questions those evaluations leave. Retain participant
material when needed to examine or reproduce an observation; do not promote
it into a domain model or governance practice automatically.

Small comparisons cannot establish a general effect across agents or tasks.
Use them to discover discriminating observations and improve the next test.
The phase may end with supported hypotheses, rejected hypotheses or a clearer
account of why the original question cannot yet be answered. No message board,
agent platform, mandatory narration format or communication rule is selected.
