# Shared Understanding in Development

## Purpose

Find out whether experiments on incremental planning and communication yield
insight useful to this application's development. They may not. A well-founded
negative or inconclusive result is a valid outcome.

We want to understand how an agent's working account becomes more concrete,
where a problem enters that account, and whether a restrained challenge lets
the agent diagnose it and change its own course.

## How we will proceed

- Begin with a brief account of the question and actions the team will take.
- Refine one bounded experimental task and the observations needed to examine it.
- Observe incremental planning, first without substantive intervention and then
  with diagnostic challenges.
- Evaluate what the observations establish and whether further experimentation
  is worth doing.

## Where we stand

The experimental approach is unproven. We have agreed the two trial directions;
we have not yet selected the common task or run either trial.

The earlier unmerged Planning with a Case Expert run is reference material.
Its proposed domain model and practices are not adopted. No product changes
or mandatory development habits are selected by this phase.

After the first milestone, the owner and advisor will reassess the phase.
There is no committed sequence of later experiments or milestones.
