# Shared Understanding in Development Roadmap

## Current direction

Evolving Workspace Accounts is concluded at its bounded result after PR #207.
This phase begins with one experiment attached to actual product planning.
Subsequent candidates change through owner feedback, participant feedback and
what the experiments reveal.

## 1. Planning with a Case Expert — planned

[Plan](milestones/returning-planner.md).

A returning planning agent models how statements and assumptions contribute to
one student-loan tax conclusion, and how applicable responsibilities remain
represented without establishing their truth. It
expresses its current understanding, stakes and intended team actions while
working with a case expert. Observe whether it recognizes a consequential gap,
locates a misunderstanding within its own plan, and corrects its course. Produce
a reviewed working model and a development action plan that future work can
use. Establish the expert's competence before relying on the exchange.

## 2. Use correspondence during a technical build — candidate

Try the useful practices from the first milestone while building one small
product change. Examine whether a builder brings consequential uncertainty to
the expert before implementing an assumption. Test whether the participants
recognize the stakes and revise their own plans, rather than merely receiving
a repair prescription. Select the task after the first experiment.

If the first exchange reveals missed messages, unanswered questions or an
expert unable to notice a changed premise, this milestone may instead prototype
the smallest channel or trigger that addresses the observed problem. That need
determines whether a repository message board is useful.

## 3. Carry understanding to new participants — candidate

Try a short plain-language account of the relevant purpose and lessons with
new participants. Examine whether they can apply it to a different task, where
they still need the original evidence, and whether it helps them question a
plausible but unsupported interpretation. Planning and governance explanations
may emerge here; rewriting documents is not itself the goal.

## Revisit these decisions

Does direct access to an expert change the work, or reproduce the old review
loop in another channel? When does an expert need to intervene? What must a
returning participant know to ask a consequential question? Which part needs a
transport or trigger, and which part needs a better shared account of the product?

Answer with observed episodes and consumer consequences. A useful first result
may justify combining, replacing or dropping either later candidate.
