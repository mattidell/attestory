# Shared Understanding in Development Roadmap

## Current direction

The phase now proceeds through explicit experiments. The previous unmerged
Planning with a Case Expert branch remains reference material; its domain model
and proposed practices are not carried forward as adopted products.

## 1. Testing What a Working Model Helps an Agent Notice — planned

[Plan](milestones/working-model-experiment.md).

Construct and examine a hypothesis about making relationships explicit in a
working model. Run a small comparison on a consequential statement-scope task,
observe the agents' inquiries and plans, and evaluate what the model presentation
can and cannot explain. Models and participant proposals remain experimental
material. The result is an assessment of the hypothesis with its evidence.

## Subsequent experiments — contingent

Choose the next experiment from what the first cannot discriminate. Possibilities
include a second task, a replication with new participants, or a comparison of
interventions that expose a tension versus provide a direct correction. These
are not committed milestones.

Before selecting one, state what uncertainty it will reduce, what observation
would change the present interpretation, and why that trial could obtain it.
Do not turn a promising observation into a mandatory development habit. Do not
build a channel until delivery or response behavior is the question being tested.

## Reference run

`milestone/returning-planner-case-expert-dialogue`, at `35b87905`, preserves the
earlier run's setup, participant accounts and proposed conclusions. It is
unmerged and is not the starting implementation line for this milestone.
Its limitations help design the replacement: the planner was given much of the
framework; expert-held evidence arrived late; final model repair became the
main activity; and producing an adopted model displaced hypothesis evaluation.

Those are observations to examine, not universal findings about agents or models.
