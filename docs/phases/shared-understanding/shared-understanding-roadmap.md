# Shared Understanding in Development Roadmap

## Current question

Will observing incremental planning and restrained course-correction challenges
yield insight worth using in this application's development?

## First milestone

[Where Incremental Planning Goes Off Course](milestones/working-model-experiment.md).

- Establish one bounded planning task and its evidence.
- Observe where problems enter a plan as the planner makes it more concrete.
- Observe a fresh planner on that task with restrained reviewer intervention.
- Evaluate what the two trials reveal, including their limitations.

This is the initial direction, not a finished experimental protocol.
The task and methods will become concrete through owner/advisor planning.

## What follows

Undecided. Review whether the experiments yielded useful observations before
adding another milestone. The phase can conclude if this approach does not
yield insight or if the trials do not answer the question well enough.
