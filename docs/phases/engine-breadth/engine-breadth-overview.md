# Engine Breadth Phase — Overview

Audience: Product (scope and exit criteria are Shared)

## Purpose

The Engine Breadth phase expands the class of 2025 federal returns the engine
can compute. Foundation established the governed substrate. Real Return proved
that a bounded slice could operate on real data, explain itself, and reach a
human surface. This phase spends that substrate on additional tax coverage.

Its standing test is:

> Does this milestone turn a previously honest-blocked class of valid return
> into an end-to-end computed result without weakening closure, citation,
> contribution, or data-boundary guarantees?

## Starting point

The closed Real Return slice covers W-2 wages, taxable interest within its
declared universe, ordinary and qualified dividends from Form 1099-DIV boxes
1a/1b, core return-level calculations, and Schedule B. Its maturity matrix is
complete at L3 or better within those columns, but that matrix is deliberately
breadth-limited.

The breadth inventory records three live content fronts:

- Form 1099-DIV box 2a capital-gain distributions are recorded but
  non-composable. The engine raises a contradiction signal but has no route to
  Form 1040 line 7a or into the QDCG computation.
- Further positive interest sources and subtractive adjustments remain outside
  line 2b's declared universe.
- Schedule B is the only implemented attachment. Schedule D is proven as an
  ontology-compatible paper stub, but no Schedule D tax content exists.

Infrastructure hardening, historical migration, authority separation, UI work,
and presentation-session follow-ups remain real work, but they are not engine
breadth.

## Scope

- Add coherent vertical slices from authoritative facts through identity,
  closure, computation, adoption, explanation, and presentation.
- Prefer named honest blocks and declared-universe exclusions whose retirement
  produces a user-visible Form 1040 line, schedule, or materially wider source
  family.
- Preserve existing accepted contracts unless prototype evidence establishes a
  necessary successor contract.
- Use production-shaped synthetic fixtures as the default completion evidence.
  A real-data operation is separately selected and never inferred from synthetic
  completion.
- Maintain the phase coverage frontier at milestone boundaries.

## Non-goals

- No breadth measured by file count, number of schemas, or number of accepted
  input fields.
- No weakening of honest blocking to claim broader coverage.
- No generic “support capital gains” claim from one bounded capital-gain path.
- No automatic inclusion of security hardening, historical migration,
  authority-separation, UI, filing, or transmission work.
- No personal facts, real documents, real outputs, workspace locations, or
  descriptive real-run evidence in the repository or planning record.
- No reopening the closed Real Return maturity matrix. It remains the historical
  instrument of that phase.

## Selection instrument

`coverage-frontier.md` is this phase's selection instrument. A candidate is a
vertical slice, not a form-box checklist. Each row names:

- the previously unsupported valid return class;
- the authoritative source or declaration surface;
- the downstream Form 1040 line or attachment;
- the current honest blocker;
- the unresolved contract question;
- the cheapest sufficient evidence target; and
- its status.

Milestone selection is owner-held Tier 3. The foreman presents the frontier,
separates breadth from hardening, and recommends the smallest slice that changes
what the engine can compute.

## Verification baseline

Every implemented breadth slice must prove, on obviously synthetic committed
fixtures:

1. authoritative contribution and identity;
2. declared source completeness or an explicit contributable declaration;
3. publication or honest non-publication through `live_coordinate_run`;
4. exact citation and explanation pins;
5. package-resolution and schema-registry integrity;
6. downstream recomputation and supersession displacement; and
7. no new data-boundary crossing.

The plan for each milestone names its additional contract, negative, lifecycle,
and presentation evidence.

## Exit criteria

The phase is ready for an owner-held close or pivot when:

- at least one previously blocked valid-return class is implemented end to end
  and recorded on the coverage frontier;
- every completed slice has synthetic authoritative-surface evidence and no
  silent unsupported neighbor;
- the frontier distinguishes remaining breadth from hardening and migration;
  and
- the roadmap records whether the next move is another breadth slice, a
  Schedule D expansion, or a different phase.

This is a minimum evidence boundary, not a promise to stop after one milestone.
The owner may continue selecting breadth milestones while the frontier remains
the highest-value direction.

## Transition from Real Return

Real Return closed on 2026-07-28 with every cell in its bounded matrix at L3 or
better. Its matrix, roadmap, and final retrospective remain the evidence for
that close. Engine Breadth inherits the working kernel, production package,
data boundary, explanation and presentation contracts, plus the named
deferrals; it does not inherit the old matrix as its selection instrument.
