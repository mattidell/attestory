## Why 22 P0c Sentences Required Refinement

1. **`computed_zero` drawn on closed-empty returns (10 properties: `#11`, `#17`, `#18`, `#19`, `#24`, `#26`, `#31`, `#32`, `#38`, `#44`)**:
   In `packages/derivation/presentation_projection.py`, `_classify_numeric` classifies a zero publication as `computed_zero` whenever `source_leaves` (any non-derived leaf pin whose `fact_id` does not contain `".source-closure"`) is non-empty. Because the producer rules for Form 1040 lines `2a`, `2b`, `3a`, `3b`, `4b`, `7a`, `25a`, `25b`, `25c`, `Schedule D line 13`, and `Schedule 8812 line 6` pin `tax.us.2025.rounding.convention` or scope/absence facts (`no-other-dependent`, `no-f1099int-tax-exempt`, `no-sale-box4-present`, `no-other-25c-withholding`), `closure_backed_zero` is unreachable on those fields and `computed_zero` is drawn on **every closed-empty return** (where zero forms are recorded) as well as when recorded forms sum/round to zero. P0c's sentences for those 10 properties claimed that recorded forms, dependent records, adjustments, or box 2a distributions existed on returns that had none.
2. **Single-source vs dual-source `published_value` states (`#25` `form1040.line-2a`, `#47` `schedule1.line-10`)**:
   - On `form1040.line-2a`, `v83/returns/v80/carried_one_exempt_dividend/recorded` takes Path A (`no-f1099int-tax-exempt = yes`, no Form 1099-INT box 8 records, only Form 1099-DIV box 12), and `demo.t0.int8-only-and-unemp-plus-w2g` has only Form 1099-INT box 8. P0c replaced predecessor `"and/or"` with `"from the completed Form 1099-INT box 8 and Form 1099-DIV box 12 records"` and dropped whole-dollar rounding.
   - On `schedule1.line-10`, all 6 recorded lists drawing `published_value` (e.g. `v83/track0/s5/reuse_unemp_alone`) have `w2g.b1` closed-empty (`line 8b = 0`), and `demo.t0.rounding-zeros-and-w2g-only` has `f1099g.1` rounding to `0` with `w2g.b1 = 200`. P0c claimed `"including the recorded unemployment compensation and gambling winnings"` unconditionally.
3. **Non-required Schedule B on `form1040.line-2b#published_value` (`#27`)**:
   In all 7 recorded lists drawing `#27` (and `demo.t0.no-w2-interest-only`), taxable interest is $100 ($1,500 or less with no adjustments), so the Schedule B attachment on the same page is `guard_inapplicable` (not required). P0c called line 2b `"the Schedule B Part I total"` unconditionally.
4. **Incomplete income vs benefit records on `form1040.line-6b#blocked` (`#35`) and line 19 credit on `form1040.tax-after-credits#blocked` (`#42`)**:
   - In all 147 recorded lists drawing `#35`, `ssa1099.benefits` is already closed-empty and `line-6b` is blocked on required income (`capital-gains.line7a-total`). P0c changed predecessor `"or"` to `"until the benefit records and worksheet information are complete"`.
   - In `rule.form1040-tax-after-credits.v2.json`, the rule requires the derived `tax.us.2025.ctc.form1040-line19` credit and notes `"The v1 absence of line 19 is no longer read; line 17 remains declared absent"`. P0c translated the stale v1 explain and falsely claimed `"confirmation that lines 17 and 19 have no amount"`.
5. **Closed-empty absence vs per-statement inclusion on `schedule1.line-21#closure_backed_zero` (`#48`)**:
   `closure_backed_zero` on `schedule1.line-21` is reached only when `count(f1098e.box1-student-loan-interest) == 0` and `f1098e.1` is closed-empty. P0c wrote `"no student loan interest from Forms 1098-E needs to be included"`, conflating closed-empty absence with per-statement inclusion.
6. **Completeness, rounding, and 2025 instruction naming (`#13`, `#33`, `#34`, `#40`, `#41`, `#45`, `#50`)**:
   Restored Schedule 1 line 26 / line 10 completeness and line 9/11a references on `#13` (`form1040.line-10`) and `#41` (`form1040.line-8`), whole-dollar rounding and completeness on `#33` (`form1040.line-3b`), `#45` (`schedule-d.line-13`), and `#50` (`schedule1.line-7`), completeness on `#34` (`form1040.line-4b`), and the 2025 Form 1040 / instructions' own checkbox name (`"The Schedule D not required box on line 7b"`) on `#40` (`form1040.line-7b`).
