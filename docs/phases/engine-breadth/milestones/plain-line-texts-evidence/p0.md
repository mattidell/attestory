## Design comparison and cost

| Candidate | What it can repair | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content successors only | The 31 sampled form-field citizens and 4 attachment titles | Insufficient for raw support IDs, status badges and fixed banners |
| Rename symbols, IDs or pins in content | Changes the text the trace concatenates | Forbidden by the charter's preservation requirements; not a wording repair |
| Change displayed page captions while retaining underlying identities | The directly demonstrated fixed/template text | Smallest demonstrated necessary surface change; builder must stop |

A content batch for just the observed citizens would cost **at least 35
successor citizens plus one core package, registry and release**: 38 new JSON
files in one publication package, with 35 member replacements. Group the 31
line-field successors separately from the four attachment titles for review;
they can share one package. This is a lower bound, not a production bill of
materials or a settled wording design. No computational rule change is
demonstrated necessary. Broader sampling could find more citizens.

At minimum, the saved page needs display captions in `renderShared` that do
not concatenate raw identity fields, while keeping those identities for
graph routing. Fixed status/banner captions would also need plain wording
under the expanded term list. This names the necessary page surface, not a
claim that a complete child-label design is settled or that a projection
change is required. It overlaps the page surface owned by blocked-sale-pages.

For later content design, local 2025 Form 1040 instructions were checked at
the headings for lines 2a/2b and 3a/3b (printed pages 25–26), and the local
Schedule D instructions at Line 21 (printed page 14). Use names such as
tax-exempt interest, taxable interest, ordinary dividends and qualified
dividends; a zero explanation must preserve which absence or completed
calculation actually supports it. This check does not establish new answer
semantics or a finished set of replacements. Source text witnesses:
