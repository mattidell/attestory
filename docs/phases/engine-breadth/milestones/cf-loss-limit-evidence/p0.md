# P0: box C and F losses are limited and carried

Charter: `docs/reviews/cf-loss-limit/charter-p0.md`. Plan:
`docs/phases/engine-breadth/milestones/cf-loss-limit.md`. Source: branch
`milestone/cf-loss-limit-2025` at `6f82c3d7`, published core v96 (registry v94,
release v94). No production, test or content file was edited.

## Result in one paragraph

Box C and F losses are limited exactly as box A and D losses are. All 90
returns ran to `saturated` with no blocked rule and no open question; all 900
line checks match the hand values; every box C/F return matches its A/D
control on every line, disposition and question, and differs only where it
should: the C/F return attaches Form 8949 and the A/D return does not. The
2025 to 2026 carryover is not computed or shown by the engine for any box,
and that is not a C/F defect: accepted ADR-0058 Decision 7 and ADR-0060
Decision 7 forbid publishing it. **Recommendation: tests only** for the
limit. Whether to compute the 2026 carryover is a foreman/owner decision
(below), not a repair this milestone can make.

## Cases

Scripts: `cases.py` (builds and runs), `hand.py` (hand values),
`compare.py` (comparison). Outputs: `runs.json`, `comparison.json`.

Every return is the harness wage return (`sale-family-audit-evidence/audit_harness.py`,
loaded by path under a unique name): one W-2, wages 50,000, standard
deduction, plus sales. Each sale has proceeds 1,000 and basis 1,000 plus the
loss. Every return gives the honest answers a filer with only these sales
gives (`honest`, `honest_exception1`, the three other Schedule D boundaries,
no inbound carryover), taxpayer and spouse age and blindness false, and, for
married filing separately, the two separate-return answers the core asks
(lived apart all year yes; spouse's separate condition false).

| Axis | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Route | `cf`: Form 8949 no-1099-B sales (box C short-term, box F long-term); `ad`: covered sales with basis reported (box A/D, lines 1a/8a), the control |
| Shape | `st` (all short-term), `lt` (all long-term), `both` (loss split evenly, odd dollar short-term) |
| Filing status | all five the `demo.parameter.capital-loss-limit.2025` parameter names: single, married filing jointly, married filing separately, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse |
| Size | below (half the limit), at (the limit), above (limit + 2,000); limit 3,000, or 1,500 married filing separately |

2 x 3 x 5 x 3 = 90 returns, each one `live_coordinate_run`, and the saved
page rendered by the real `_render_page`. Every value is read from the model
embedded in the saved page, which is asserted equal to the presentation
model. Wall time 38 s on 9 workers.

Head of household and qualifying surviving spouse run with no qualifying
person recorded; the engine accepted the status without asking. That is out
of this milestone's scope and does not touch the loss limit.

### Where the engine computes the carryover, and where a filer sees it

- The engine computes only the **inbound** carryover, 2024 to 2025:
  `rule.capital-loss-carryover.short-term` and `.long-term` (core v96,
  version v1) run the worksheet on the five 2024 prior-return facts and feed
  Schedule D lines 6 and 14. On these returns the inbound answer is "no
  carryover", so both publish 0 and lines 6 and 14 are `computed_zero`.
- The **outbound** carryover, 2025 to 2026, is computed nowhere. No published
  symbol in any of the 90 runs names a 2026 carryover (the only carry or 2026
  symbols seen are the two inbound ones and `payments.line36-applied-to-2026`,
  the refund applied to 2026 tax). The line 21 rule says so in its own notes:
  "No 2026 carry amount" (`packages/content/tax/2025/rule.schedule-d-line21.json`
  line 16).
- That absence is binding, not an omission: ADR-0058 Decision 7
  (`docs/adr/0058-schedule-d-signed-downstream-and-line-21-limitation.md`
  line 120) and ADR-0060 Decision 7
  (`docs/adr/0060-capital-loss-carryover-worksheet-and-route.md` line 150)
  forbid publishing any amount carried into 2026, and ADR-0058 rejects
  publishing it "for completeness".
- On the saved page a filer sees line 21 labelled "Line 21 limited loss",
  explained as "the capital loss you can deduct this year, after the yearly
  limit" (form field `schedule-d.line-21` v2), and the labels of lines 6 and
  14 ("short-term / long-term capital loss carryover", both 0, which are the
  inbound lines). Nothing on the page says the excess (2,000 in the over-limit
  cases) carries to 2026. This is the same for boxes A and D.

## Recommendation

**Tests only.** No content successor is needed for the limit: box C and F
losses already reach line 21 and line 7a with the right cap under every
filing status, the same as box A and D. Track 1 pins a subset of these cases
(suggested: `cf/{st,lt,both}/{single,married_filing_separately}/{at,above}`
with their A/D controls, through `live_coordinate_run` and the saved page)
and asserts that no 2026 carry symbol is published, so the ADR boundary stays
visible in the tests.

## Decisions for the foreman

1. **The plan's "right 2026 carryover" conflicts with accepted ADRs.** The
   plan's scope and the parked item name the 2026 carryover; ADR-0058 and
   ADR-0060 Decision 7 forbid publishing it for every box. Options:
   (a) narrow this milestone to the limit and pin "no 2026 carryover" plus
   the hand values in the evidence, leaving the 2026 carryover as its own
   parked item; or (b) open a superseding ADR (an owner decision) and a
   separate milestone to compute and show the 2025 to 2026 carryover for all
   boxes. Recommended: (a). P0 does not count this as a stop, because no
   correct 2025 value is wrong; a 2026 value would need new rules, symbols and
   a form field, which this milestone's non-goals and the ADRs both exclude.
2. **Page wording.** The line 21 text does not tell a filer with an
   over-limit loss that the rest carries forward. A one-sentence content
   change to the `schedule-d.line-21` form field (a v3 successor) could say
   so without publishing an amount; whether that sentence is inside ADR-0058's
   bound is for the foreman to judge. Not recommended for this milestone
   unless (1) is decided as (a) with a wording addendum.
3. **Head of household and qualifying surviving spouse** were accepted with
   no qualifying person recorded. Outside this milestone; noted only.
