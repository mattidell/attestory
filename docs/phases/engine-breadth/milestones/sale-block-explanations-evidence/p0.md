## 1. Inventory on Published Core `v105`

### 1.1 Engine & Projection Pipeline Citations

1. **Package compilation (`packages/derivation/package_validation.py:1645-1702`):** For every `source-family.v2` declaring `member_constraints` or `identity_exclusivity`, compilation synthesizes a companion `<family_id>.member-validation.synthesized` (`rule-artifact.v5`) rule whose `blocked.missing` list starts empty and whose `meaning` stays pinned inside the source-family JSON.
2. **Runner validation (`packages/derivation/runner.py:1743-1963`):** `_evaluate_family_validation` evaluates each member's `member_constraints` (`runner.py:1815`), emits one derived finding per member (`<family_id>.member-validation.synthesized.<member_fact_id>`, `runner.py:1837-1856`) containing `{"member_fact_id": ..., "violations": [...]}`, evaluates cross-family `identity_exclusivity` (`runner.py:1863-1913`), and if any code accumulated, calls `self._record_blocked(rule, access, "FAMILY_VALIDATION_BLOCKED", sorted(set(codes)))` (`runner.py:1915`).
3. **Disposition recording (`packages/derivation/runner.py:2531-2550`):** `_record_blocked` maps internal `FAMILY_VALIDATION_BLOCKED` to `disposition_row["code"] = "DEPENDENCY_INVALID"` (`derivation-record.v8` closed record code set) and preserves `missing = sorted(set(codes))`.
4. **Presentation projection (`packages/derivation/presentation_projection.py`):**
   - `meaning_for` (`presentation_projection.py:766-782`) recognizes `artifact_id.endswith(".member-validation.synthesized")`, extracts `family_id`, and looks up `key` (`block_code`) in that family's pinned `member_constraints` (`_constraint_meanings`, `presentation_projection.py:419-438`).
   - `_closure_question_reasons` (`presentation_projection.py:819-864`) calls `explain(current, key)` (`:849`) *before* `record_input_fact` (`:860`). When `explain` finds `meaning_for(current, key)`, it adds the raw `meaning` string to `frames[-1].extras` and immediately `continue`s without recording any `input-question` or `questionGroup`.
   - `build_presentation_model` (`presentation_projection.py:5539-5585`) attaches `resolved.reasons` from closure reasons and leaves `resolved.questionRefs` empty when no `questionGroup` was recorded.

## 2. 2025 IRS Instructions (Form 8949 and Schedule D)

All official 2025 PDFs were fetched directly from IRS.gov (`2025 Instructions for Form 8949`, `i8949.pdf`; `2025 Instructions for Schedule D`, `i1040sd.pdf`). Starting from `sale-reasons-plain-evidence/wording.json`, below are the governing quotes and resulting wording + fixing-question decisions in [wording.json](wording.json):

### 2.1 Verbatim Quotes

- **Short-Term vs. Long-Term (`2025 Instructions for Form 8949`, *General Instructions -> Short-Term or Long-Term*):**
  > "Separate your capital gains and losses according to how long you held or owned the property. The holding period for short-term capital gains and losses is generally 1 year or less. ... Report these transactions on Part I of Form 8949 (or line 1a of Schedule D if you can use Exception 1 under the instructions for line 1, later). The holding period for long-term capital gains and losses is generally more than 1 year. ... Report these transactions on Part II of Form 8949 (or line 8a of Schedule D if you can use Exception 1 under the instructions for line 1, later)."
- **Basis Reported vs. Not Reported (`2025 Instructions for Form 8949`, *Specific Instructions -> Box A or Box G* & *Box B or Box H*; `2025 Instructions for Schedule D`, *Lines 1a and 8a*):**
  > "Report on Part I with box A or box G checked all short-term transactions reported to you on Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA (or substitute statement) with an amount shown for cost or other basis unless the statement indicates that amount wasn't reported to the IRS. ... Report on Part I with box B or box H checked all short-term transactions reported to you on Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA (or substitute statement) without an amount shown for cost or other basis or showing that cost or other basis wasn't reported to the IRS. If your statement shows cost or other basis for the transaction was reported to the IRS (for example, if box 12 on Form 1099-B or box 2 on Form 1099-DA is checked), see Box A or Box G above."
- **Wash Sales & Form 1099-B Box 1g (`2025 Instructions for Schedule D`, *General Instructions -> Wash Sales*):**
  > "A wash sale occurs when you sell or otherwise dispose of stock or securities (including a contract or option to acquire or sell stock or securities) at a loss and, within 30 days before or after the sale or disposition, you: 1. Buy substantially identical stock or securities... If you received a Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA (or substitute statement), box 1g of Form 1099-B or box 1i of Form 1099-DA will generally show whether there was any nondeductible wash sale loss and its amount... Enter \"W\" in column (f). Enter as a positive number in column (g) the amount of the loss not allowed."
- **Incorrect Wash-Sale Loss & Zeroing Out (`2025 Instructions for Form 8949`, *Column (f) -> Code W*):**
  > "Report the sale or exchange on Form 8949 and enter the amount of the nondeductible loss as a positive number in column (g). ... If you received a Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA (or substitute statement) and the amount of nondeductible wash sale loss shown in box 1g on Form 1099-B or box 1i on Form 1099-DA is incorrect, enter the correct amount of the nondeductible loss as a positive number in column (g). ... If no part of the loss is a nondeductible loss from a wash sale transaction, enter -0- in column (g)."
- **Code B Basis Correction (`2025 Instructions for Form 8949`, *Column (f) -> Code B*; `2025 Instructions for Schedule D`, *Lines 1a and 8a*):**
  > "You received a Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA (or substitute statement) and the basis shown in box 1e on Form 1099-B or box 1g on Form 1099-DA is incorrect ... If this transaction is reported on Part I with box A or box G checked at the top or if this transaction is reported on Part II with box D or box J checked at the top, enter the basis shown on Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA (or substitute statement) in column (e), even though that basis is incorrect. Correct the error by entering an adjustment in column (g)."

## 3. Design Comparison: Two Homes

| Dimension | **Home 1 (Recommended): `family-block-explanation.v1` citizen + `presentation-model.v3`** | **Home 2: `presentation-model.v4` successor field** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What is published** | New schema `family-block-explanation.v1`, package schema `artifact-package.v44`, 30 explanation citizens (plus 6 missing `*-txn.adjustment` `input-question.v1` citizens), all pinned inside `package.core-calculations.v106`. **No `source-family` file changes.** | `presentation-model.v4.schema.json` adds structured block-explanation / fixing-question fields directly on `resolved.reasons` or `questionGroups`; `presentation_projection.py` + `citation-walk.v1.html` updated; explanation text either sits in projection/tax Python or still needs content citizens. |
| **What a saved record pins** | Saved sale assertions stay pinned to their exact existing `source-family.v2` version (`v1` / `v2`); the run record pins `package.core-calculations.v106`.
