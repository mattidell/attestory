- `tests/test_eic_answer_questions_v100.py` (live): the real coordinator and
  saved page on published v99 and v100. For each of the four answers, and for
  each filing status where it is required (single, qualifying surviving
  spouse, unmarried head of household, joint; spouse SSN on joint returns with
  and without a child), the answer is left out of the record. v99 and v100

  once show every question. Answered returns compute exactly as v99, with hand

  nothing and match v99.

  loader entries carry v99.
