## 1. Trace: what happens today on core v96

**Box 13 cannot be recorded.** No fact type on core v96 carries a Form 1099-B
box 13 amount or a bartering yes/no. The Form 1099-B statement-level facts are
box 4 withholding reported (`packages/content/tax/2025/f1099b-statement-boxes.bundle.json:9`),
box 7 (`f1099b-statement-boxes.bundle.json:39`), the box 4 amount (`family.f1099b-b4.json`)
and the no-sale box 4 pair (`input-question.f1099b-no-sale-box4-present.v1.json:9`).
A search of `packages/content/` and `packages/tax/loader.py` for "barter",
"box 13" and "box13" finds only Form 1099-DIV box 13 (specified private
activity bond interest, `packages/tax/loader.py:299`). The statement and
broker entities (`tax.us.f1099b-statement`, `tax.us.f1099b-broker`) can be
introduced with nothing on them, so the recording surface for "a Form 1099-B
from a barter exchange" exists; only the amount has no fact type. The
statement-level questions are asked only of statements "you recorded a sale
from" (`input-question.f1099b-statement-box7-loss-not-allowed.json:9`), and a
barter exchange's statement usually has no sale.

**No question names bartering.** The answers that can refuse it are the
Schedule 1 Part I scope answers that `rule.schedule1-line10` v3 requires and
compares to "yes" on its only computing path
(`rule.schedule1-line10.v3.json:47`, `:51`, `:175`, `:239`):

- `schedule1-part1-scope.no-sch1-line3-business` ("No Schedule 1 line 3
  business income or loss is present", `schedule1-part1-scope.bundle.json:54`),
  asked by the sentence that the five lines 2a-6 facts share: "In 2025, did you
  receive alimony, or have income or loss from a business, a farm, rental
  property or royalties, a partnership, S corporation or trust, or any other
  gain or loss reported on Schedule 1 lines 2a through 6?"
  (`input-question.schedule1-part1-scope-no-sch1-line3-business.v1.json:7`);
- `w2g-scope.no-sch1-other-income-except-w2g`, asked "Besides gambling
  winnings on a Form W-2G you have recorded, did you have any other income that
  goes on Schedule 1 lines 8a through 8z (for example ...)?"
  (`input-question.w2g-scope-no-sch1-other-income-except-w2g.v1.json:7`).

Form 1040 line 8 reads Schedule 1 line 10 (`rule.form1040-line8.json:30`) and
line 9 reads line 8 (`rule.form1040-line9.v8.json:9`), so every complete
return answers both questions. Neither sentence names bartering or box 13.

**Runs** (`probe-v96.json`; single filer, wages 50,000, 4,500 withheld, every
sale family open and closed by honest answers):

| Case | Schedule 1 line 10 | Form 1040 line 9 | Line 24 | Line 34 / 37 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| control/base (no Form 1099-B) | 0 | 50,000 | 3,875 | refund 625 |
| alone/as-asked (box 13 only, questions answered no) | 0 | 50,000 | 3,875 | refund 625 |
| zero/as-asked (box 13 of 0) | 0 | 50,000 | 3,875 | refund 625 |
| sale/as-asked (box 13 beside a 20,000 short-term gain) | 0 | 70,000 | 6,855 | owe 2,355 |
| alone/business (lines 2a-6 question yes) | guard_inapplicable | blocked | blocked | blocked |
| alone/business-all (all five facts yes) | guard_inapplicable | blocked | blocked | blocked |
| alone/other-income (lines 8a-8z question yes) | guard_inapplicable | blocked | blocked | blocked |
| sale/business | guard_inapplicable | blocked | blocked | blocked |
| alone/unanswered (lines 2a-6 facts not answered) | blocked, page asks the question | blocked | blocked | blocked |

The box-13-only return is identical, value for value and blocked row for
blocked row, to the return with no Form 1099-B at all, and to box 13 of 0.
The engine cannot tell a box 13 of 0 from a box 13 of any amount. **A filer
who answers the questions as worded ("no") gets a complete return with the
bartering income silently left out**: Form 1040 line 9 shows 50,000 and a
refund of 625 whatever box 13 says. A filer who knows that bartering is
business income (Pub. 525) and answers the lines 2a-6 question "yes" is
refused honestly: line 10 is guard_inapplicable and lines 8, 9, 11, 15, 16,
24, 33, 34 and 37 are blocked. The page then shows for line 10 only "No value
is published because a completeness or guard condition is not satisfied."
(`schedule1.line-10.form-field.v5.json:28`) and for line 8 "Form 1040 line 8
is blocked because Schedule 1 line 10 is incomplete or unavailable." Nothing
on the page names bartering.

So the defect is in the question, not in the computation: the refusing answer
exists and is required on every return, but its words do not reach a filer
holding a box 13 amount.

**Where box 13 goes.** It is not a Schedule D or Form 8949 amount. It is
ordinary income: generally Schedule C line 1 (then Schedule 1 line 3), in
some cases Schedule E, and otherwise Schedule 1 line 8z. **To compute it** the
engine would need Schedule C (gross receipts, expenses, net profit), Schedule
SE and the Schedule 1 line 15 deduction, the effect on the qualified business
income deduction and on earned income for the EIC, or a Schedule 1 line 8z
producer for a barter that is not a business. All are non-goals of this
milestone. A box 4 amount on a barter statement is already reachable through
the no-sale box 4 question (Form 1040 line 25b); the return is refused anyway
once the bartering is answered.

## 3. Bounds

- No accepted ADR names bartering or Form 1099-B box 13 (ADR index digests;
  a search of `docs/adr/` for "barter" and "box 13" outside Form 1099-DIV
  finds nothing).
- The bound that applies is the Schedule 1 Part I absence authority: line 10
  v3 computes only while every unsupported Part I class is answered absent.
  Its notes say "Winnings without a Form W-2G and business gambling are refused
  by that answer" (`rule.schedule1-line10.v3.json`, `notes`); the fact titles
  say a "no" "asserts it is present and blocks the bounded additional-income
  claim" (`schedule1-part1-scope.bundle.json`). This is the ADR-0016 Decision 4
  completeness pattern (absence facts), applied to Part II by ADR-0065.
- Sale-family audit N2 recorded only that the refusing answer "is not
  established". It is now established: `no-sch1-line3-business` (and
  `no-sch1-other-income-except-w2g` for a non-business barter).

## 4. Design

**Recommended (variant q): name bartering in the existing Schedule 1 lines
2a-6 question.** Five input-question successors, one per fact that shares
the sentence (line 2a alimony, line 3 business, line 4 other gains, line 5
rental, line 6 farm), each v1 to v2, with the same sentence:

> In 2025, did you receive alimony, or have income or loss from a business, a
> farm, rental property or royalties, a partnership, S corporation or trust,
> or any other gain or loss reported on Schedule 1 lines 2a through 6? Also
> answer yes if a Form 1099-B you received shows an amount above zero in box 13
> (Bartering), even if you do not think of it as a business.

No fact type, rule, family, form field, requirement or answer changes. A filer
with a box 13 amount answers yes, which records the existing
`no-sch1-line3-business` "no", and the return is refused by Schedule 1 line 10
exactly as it is today for any business income. A box 13 of 0 (or blank) is
"no" and changes nothing. The five stay identical so the page still draws one
sentence (`probe-v97-q.json`, alone/unanswered).

**Optional (variant qf): q plus a Schedule 1 line 10 form field successor (v5
to v6)** whose guard_inapplicable sentence names the causes, bartering among
them:

> Schedule 1 line 10 has no value because one of these is true: your Form
> 1099-G or Form 1099-K statements are not confirmed complete, or you said you
> have Schedule 1 income this return does not compute (alimony; business
> income, including an amount in box 13 (Bartering) of a Form 1099-B; other
> gains; rental, royalty, partnership, S corporation or trust income; farm
> income; other income on lines 8a through 8z; a state or local tax refund;
> another Form 1099-G payment; or an unemployment repayment). Your total income
> is not shown, so that income is not left out.

The field is embedded in every saved page, so qf changes that sentence and the
field version on every successful recorded list. Whether an unclosed Form
1099-G or Form 1099-K source set reaches guard_inapplicable (rather than
blocked) was not established; the first clause is there so the sentence is a
true disjunction either way.

**Rejected shapes.**

- A per-statement box 13 yes/no (as box 4 and box 7 are asked): those
  questions reach only statements with a recorded sale, and a barter
  exchange's statement usually has none, so the main case would never be
  asked.
- A new return-level box 13 answer read by line 10 (or any rule on the line 9
  path): it adds a required answer to every return. All 775 recorded lists
  lack it, so every one would stop computing; the charter forbids changing
  which answer an existing path needs except to refuse a return that is wrong,
  and no recorded list carries a box 13 amount (section 5). (Reasoned from the
  requirement, not run.)
- A box 13 amount fact the package never consumes: it would surface in
  `unsupportedSourceFindings` but the return would still compute, so the
  income is still left out of the numbers.
- Naming bartering only in the lines 8a-8z question: Pub. 525 sends bartering
  generally to Schedule C, so listing it as an 8z example misstates where it
  goes.

**Prototype.** `build.py` copies the published content and releases outside
the repository, writes the successors (root version and declared prose only;
undoing them recovers each predecessor byte for byte), and closes the set
over `{"id", "version"}` pins: nothing pins these questions or the field, so
the closure is the declared set (q: 5 member and 5 entrypoint replacements;
qf: 6 members, 5 entrypoints). `generate_to_directory` validated the package,
registry and release for both (`build-q.json`, `build-qf.json`). The loader
entries are copied from v96 for the run (`loader_from`).

**Probe on the successors** (`probe-v97-q.json`, `probe-v97-qf.json`): every
line value and every blocked rule of all nine cases is the same as on v96.
On q, the only change a filer sees is the question sentence on the
unanswered case. On qf, the business, business-all, other-income and
sale/business cases also show the new line 10 sentence.

## 5. Replay

`replay.py` over `tools/recorded_replay.py` `run_full`: the 775 recorded
lists, published v96 against the scratch v97, strict full-surface
comparison, nine workers, four shards per variant (about 98 s of replay per
shard).

**Census** (`census.json`): no recorded list carries a box 13 or bartering
fact (there is no such fact type), and all 775 answer the five lines 2a-6
facts "yes" and the lines 8a-8z answer "yes". No recorded return is silently
wrong by this milestone's test, and none can show the question.

| Variant | Lists | Pass | Unexpected | Classes | Declared changes seen | Unused expectations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| q (`replay-q.json`) | 775 | 775 | 0 | 691 expected changes, 84 identical | package version v96 to v97 only | 6: the question sentence and the five question versions |
| qf (`replay-qf.json`) | 775 | 775 | 0 | 691 expected changes, 84 identical | package version; line 10 guard sentence and field version v5 to v6 on all 691 | the same 6 |

The 84 identical lists are recorded lists that fail on both cores with the
same error class and message (`error/error`). On q no published value, page
sentence, attachment, graph node or report row changed on any recorded list.
The unused expectations are honest: a question is saved only where the page
asks it, and no recorded list leaves it unanswered; the input-question version
is not on any saved surface. The probe lists exercise them
(`replay-probe-q.json`: 9 of 9 pass, 29 question-sentence records on
alone/unanswered; `replay-probe-qf.json`: 9 of 9 pass, plus the line 10
sentence on all nine). The five question-version expectations are unused there
too, because no saved surface records an input question's version.

## 6. Recommendation

Publish variant q as core v97, registry v95, release v95: five input-question
successors, content only. It changes no number on any recorded return, adds
no requirement, and makes the existing refusing answer reach a filer holding
a box 13 amount. Pin it in Track 1 with live tests over the nine probe cases
(at least alone/as-asked answered yes after the new question, alone/unanswered
showing the new sentence, and box 13 beside a sale), plus the recorded-list
replay. No engine, schema, projection, page or kernel change is needed.

## 7. Decisions for the foreman

1. **Variant q or qf.** q is the smallest change and leaves every saved page
   on the recorded lists identical apart from the package version. qf also
   names bartering where the refusal shows, at the cost of a changed sentence
   and field version on every saved page and a disjunction whose first clause
   was not shown to be reachable.
2. **Five successors or one.** The five lines 2a-6 facts share one sentence.
   Changing only the line 3 question (one successor) also refuses honestly
   but makes an unanswered page draw two near-identical sentences.
3. **A non-business barter is refused through the business fact.** A filer
   whose barter belongs on line 8z or Schedule E still answers this question
   yes ("even if you do not think of it as a business"), so the recorded fact
   says line 3 income is present when it is line 8z or line 5 income. Nothing
   reads that fact except line 10 and the Schedule 1 attachment, both of which
   only refuse; no number is wrong. Accept, or also name bartering in the
   lines 8a-8z question (one more successor).
4. **"Above zero".** The sentence asks about an amount above zero so a blank
   or 0 box 13 is a "no"; the instructions forbid negative amounts.
5. **Track 1 test scope**: the nine probe lists as live pins on v97 (and
   reuse on v96), plus the recorded-list replay as the neighbour check.
