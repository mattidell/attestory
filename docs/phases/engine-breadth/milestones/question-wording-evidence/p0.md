Charter: `docs/reviews/question-wording/charter-p0.md`. Plan:
`docs/phases/engine-breadth/milestones/question-wording.md`. Measured on
published core v86 (registry v84, release v84) and on a scratch core v87

## Recommendation

All three items can be fixed by changing words alone. No rule, schema,
expression operation, selection or recording surface changes. Under the
proposed words, every probe case reaches its hand value on v87 (25 of 25).
All 298 recorded returns give identical pins on v86 and v87. Two current
wordings give a wrong number when answered truthfully: the age title, and
the student-loan question on a joint return. The wash-sale wording leaves a
truthful filer blocked. Take all three items. The decisions are at the end.

No item hit a stop condition. Three findings sit beside the items and are
not fixed by words. Each one would need an engine, selection or presentation
change, so they are reported here and left out of the prototype: findings
1, 2 and 3 under "Findings outside the words".

## Law (2025; `law.json`, extract line ranges)

- **Code W** (Form 8949 instructions, lines 674-690): code W is "a
  nondeductible loss from a wash sale". Enter "the amount of the
  nondeductible loss as a positive number in column (g)", and if no part of
  the loss is a wash-sale loss, "enter -0- in column (g)". Schedule D
  instructions, "Wash Sales" (386-430): enter "W" in column (f) and "the
  amount of the loss not allowed" in column (g). So the adjustment belongs
  only to sales that have a wash-sale loss.
- **Exception 1** (Form 1040 instructions, line 7a, 2031-2051): "If you sold
  a capital asset ... you must complete and attach Form 8949 and Schedule D.
  Exception 1. You don't have to file Form 8949 or Schedule D if ... 1. You
  have no capital losses, and your only capital gains are capital gain
  distributions from Form(s) 1099-DIV, box 2a ... 2. None of the Form(s)
  1099-DIV ... have an amount in box 2b ... 2c ... or 2d". Line 7b
  (2147-2178): check "Schedule D not required" only if Exception 1 applies.
  Exception 2 (2052-2058 and 2117-2146) covers covered sales with basis
  reported and no column (f) or (g) entry: Schedule D, not Exception 1.
- **Age 65** (line 12d, 2183-2191): "born before January 2, 1961, or were
  blind at the end of 2025". **Death in 2025** (2128-2151): if the spouse
  "was born before January 2, 1961, but died in 2025 before reaching age 65,
  don't check the box". A person "is considered to reach age 65 on the day
  before the person's 65th birthday". In the instruction's example, the
  spouse was born February 14, 1960. A death on February 13, 2025 counts as
  65; a death on February 12, 2025 does not. Schedule 1-A Part V
  (7876-7931) applies the same test to the senior deduction ($6,000 per
  person; MAGI thresholds $75,000 and $150,000).
- **Blindness** (2158-2179): not totally blind on December 31, 2025 requires
  an eye doctor's certified statement of 20/200 or worse in the better eye
  with glasses, or a field of 20 degrees or less. Dependents worksheet
  (2288-2312): $1,600 per box, $2,000 if single or head of household.
- **Student-loan interest** (Form 1040 Schedule 1 line 21, 7062-7080): "You,
  or your spouse if filing jointly, aren't claimed as a dependent on someone
  else's ... 2025 tax return". Publication 970 page 33 (2256-2271): "No one
  else is claiming you as a dependent". This is an actual claim, not "can be
  claimed".

## Findings outside the words (not fixed here)

1. **The mixed wash-sale case asks nothing.** Take a return with a recorded
   covered wash sale and the adjustment family open. Line 1b then waits on
   the closure and on the wash transaction's admission check
   (`DEPENDENCY_INVALID`), and that check has no question. The page then
   shows no question on line 1b or below it (`wash-st-unclosed`,
   `wash-lt-unclosed`). Fixing this is a presentation or engine change.
   Better words reach only the plain-sale case.
2. **Nobody is asked for age or blindness.** The four booleans default to
   false and no input question names them. A filer who never states them
   gets the under-65, sighted result with no prompt. The titles are not
   shown on any page measured, including `demographic-titles` with all four
   set true. The proposed titles fix what the fact says, not what a filer
   reads. Asking would be a new question and a change to who is asked, which
   is a non-goal.
3. **The false Exception 1 "no" refusal stays parked**, as the plan says.

## Decisions for the foreman

1. **Wash-sale words:** take the sibling-parallel v4 text. This is
   recommended.
2. **Exception 1:** take "and that you did not sell stock, funds or other
   property in 2025 (even a sale with no gain or loss)". No number moves.
   The answer now matches the conclusion that line 7b v2 already enforces
   when a sale is recorded. The fact's bundle title (`exception1.bundle.json`,
   C1: "the return's only capital gains are ... box-2a") does not mention
   sales. Decide whether a title successor is in scope or whether the
   question alone carries the meaning.
3. **Student-loan question:** add the spouse clause. This is recommended,
   because it is the Form 1040 line 21 test and fixes the joint case. The
   fact title in `sli-scope.bundle.json` still says "the filer". Decide
   whether to retitle it in the same bundle successor.
4. **Age and blindness:** the only text is the fact title, which no page
   shows (finding 2). Either take the title successor (vocabulary bundle
   v3) as a meaning fix, or drop the item and record finding 2 on the
   roadmap. The blindness titles go beyond the plan's three defects. Keep
   them only if the bundle successor is taken anyway.
5. **Loader entries:** a real core v87 needs its keyed entries in
   `packages/tax/loader.py`: the companion declaration and the covered-flag
   refusal map. Every core since v80 has added them. The prototype supplies
   them in memory (`loader_from` v86). They are code outside "content only",
   so confirm they are in Track 1's scope.
6. **Finding 1:** whether to put it on the roadmap's parked list.
