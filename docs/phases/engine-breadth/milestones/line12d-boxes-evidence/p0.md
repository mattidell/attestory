## Law (`trace.json` → `law`)

Reused from `age-blindness-asked-evidence/law.json`. Its sha256
`1d9bdf9e…ea8bc5` is checked by `trace.py`. The law says to check the boxes
for you and, on a joint return, for your spouse. Never check spouse boxes on
head of household. On a separate return, check spouse boxes only if the
spouse had no income, isn't filing, and can't be claimed as a dependent
(the condition fact). The chart's qualifying surviving spouse rows stop at
two boxes, so only the taxpayer's boxes count there.

## Design (`prototype.py`, `prototype-build.json`)

The design is content only. It adds 17 members to package v90 (registry and
release v88): `citation.form1040.line-12d`, plus one checked/not-checked pair
per box. Each pair is two computation rules (`rule.form1040-line12d-<box>`
and `-not-checked`) and two `form-field.v3` fields
(`form1040.line-12d-<box>` and `-not-checked`), where the boxes are
`you-age`, `you-blind`, `spouse-age` and `spouse-blind`. The you boxes
publish on every status. The spouse boxes publish only on a joint return or
on a separate return whose condition fact is yes. On every other status both
fields of a spouse box are `guard_inapplicable` ("hidden"). No engine,
schema, projection or renderer change was made.

## Recommendation

Do not build the prototype as it stands. The numbers are safe so far, but
the page would trade a missing line for two broken lines on every return.
Choose one of these:

1. **Smallest renderer change (recommended).** In `citation-walk.v1.html`
   `renderLine`, let the categorical branch also draw `not_checked` (as "not
   checked" with its explain and citation) instead of throwing. Keep the
   prototype content unchanged. This also repairs line 6d's existing alert.
   It does not repair line 6c's `not_elected`, unless the change is widened
   to a declared closed set of categorical render strings. It is a
   presentation change with its own browser observation. Whether the page
   is versioned history that needs a successor is for the foreman to
   establish.
2. **Content only, one field per box.** Drop the not-checked fields. The
   box is `checked` or `guard_inapplicable`, and the renderer prints "Not
   applicable." with a static explain. That avoids every alert, but an
   unchecked "you" box reads "Not applicable.", which is wrong in kind: the
   box applies and is empty. It also cannot tell "answer no" apart from
   "this status does not check spouse boxes". This is not recommended.
