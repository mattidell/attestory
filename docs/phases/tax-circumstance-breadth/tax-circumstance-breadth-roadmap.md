# Tax Circumstance Breadth Roadmap

## Opening milestone — Mortgage Interest and What the Loan Paid For — closed 2026-10-15

[Plan](milestones/mortgage-interest-circumstances.md) ·
[Retrospective](../../milestone-retrospectives/2026-10-15-mortgage-interest-circumstances.md).
Delivered: one current Form 1098 statement records ordinary answers about the
home borrowing, calculates the bounded interest treatment, and saves an
explanation the person can correct. Uncertainty and known unsupported cases
are named and do not produce an amount.

Plan a bounded extension of the existing Form 1098 route. Let a person identify
the home and borrowing and describe what the borrowed money paid for. Use those
circumstances to establish the relevant tax treatment and carry it to Schedule A
with an explanation the person can inspect and correct.

The existing route already connects Form 1098 to the deduction calculation,
but asks for tax-shaped confirmations such as acquisition-debt use. That makes
this a useful first application: an existing consumer can test the translation,
and the prior student-loan work supplies concrete mechanisms to examine.
Home, debt, use of proceeds and interest remain distinct concepts.

Investigate the bounded case before selecting the implementation. Systematic
handling of assumptions belongs in that investigation wherever the case needs
it; a separate general assumption framework is not a prerequisite by decree.

## Contrasting milestone — Personal-Item Sales: What Was Sold and What the Payment Means — closed 2026-10-17

[Plan](milestones/personal-item-sale-circumstances.md) ·
[Retrospective](../../milestone-retrospectives/2026-10-17-personal-item-sale-circumstances.md).
Delivered: one Form 1099-K for one personally used item bought and sold at a
loss enters its Box 1a amount at the top of 2025 Schedule 1, from an ordinary
account the person records and corrects, with a saved explanation of the
computation. Gain, break-even, a second statement and other outcomes are
named and enter nothing. The owner narrowed the route to the loss after P0
showed gain reporting needs Form 8949 capability.

Opening intent: investigate one
personally used item, one identifiable sale and one Form 1099-K statement.
Connect reported receipts to what was sold and its cost, distinguish gain from
a nondeductible personal loss, and develop the supported reporting and saved
explanation path. This moves beyond loan eligibility into the character and
amount of income. Exact 2025 treatment, reporting integration and the boundary
with business activity remain P0 questions, not settled implementation choices.

A bounded retirement-distribution circumstance remains an alternative if P0
shows the selected sale route needs disproportionate prerequisite work. Return
that choice to the owner rather than quietly changing the goal or broadening
the milestone into a general capital-asset or business system.

## Consolidation — More Than One Statement: Keeping Each Account With Its Form — closed 2026-10-19

[Plan](milestones/multiple-statement-circumstances.md) ·
[Retrospective](../../milestone-retrospectives/2026-10-19-multiple-statement-circumstances.md).
Delivered: any number of Forms 1099-K, each with its own recorded, correctable
and removable sale account, reason and saved explanation. When every current
statement is a supported personal loss, their Box 1a amounts are combined
once at the top of 2025 Schedule 1. ADR 0079 adds a recorded family-version
handover. The owner kept the one-statement Form 1098 limit; mortgage breadth
needs debt and home identity across loans.

## Bounded extension — One Home Loan, More Than One Interest Statement — closed 2026-10-27

[Plan](milestones/mortgage-statement-borrowing.md) ·
[Lessons](../../milestone-retrospectives/2026-10-25-mortgage-statement-borrowing.md).

The owner returned to product work for one continuing original home borrowing
described by one or two Forms 1098. The implementation keeps the reports and
their local answers separate, records explicit shared borrowing answers and
links, calculates one bounded result and saves its actual support chain.
Correction, withdrawal and explicit clear have different scopes. A narrow
member-restoration successor lets a fresh exact source become usable without
reviving prior findings or completeness claims.

General multiple-loan/home support, refinancing, allocation, higher
grandfathered debt limits and browser observation are not selected. This
extension does not change the earlier phase assessment or require another
vertical. Further milestone selection remains owner-held.
