## Current contract (provisional)

This is a production-design proposal, not an accepted contract or implementation. Delivery is bounded to the selected two-report same-borrowing case, with one-report and closed-empty compatibility transitions. It does not generalize to three or more reports or borrowing groups. A report remains its own source and keeps all nine ordinary answers on its current `payer + statement + tax-year` identity. The connected account is an additional, explicit ordinary claim that the two exact reports concern one continuing borrowing. It does not merge reports, copy answers between them, establish deductibility, or infer identity from lender, account string, property, amount, date, or matching answers.

### Current-state route and calculation

The production candidate is one successor mortgage line8a producer, not two producers whose symbols collide. It retains the externally bound symbol `tax.us.2025.schedule-a.line8a` and the existing `family.f1098.json` closure claim. The route is selected from current facts and findings, not from a remembered positive answer or the existence of a stale borrowing entity:

| Current state | Expected route/result |
| --- | --- |
| F1098 family genuinely closed-empty | Preserve existing closed-empty line8a authority: zero. Retained account, ceiling or other-debt claims alone do not defeat it. |
| One current F1098 report, no current connected-membership activity | Preserve `rule.f1098-line8a.v2.json`'s existing qualified and determined-zero reason branches and its current explanation. The nine report-local answers still determine whether the singleton is qualified, zero, or unresolved; contrary answers are not uniformly a block. |
| Two current F1098 reports with affirmative membership links to the same current borrowing | Select connected readiness. Require exactly two current link outcomes keyed to these source reports, current account support and both report-local results. Do not fall back to singleton if one link/support is absent, contrary, cannot-tell or withdrawn. |
| Connected route: both report reasons qualified and shared support passes | Publish one readiness result; the single line8a producer collects both authorized current Box 1 members exactly once. |
| Connected route: any member's reason is determined zero, unresolved, unsupported, or contradictory | Do not sum a favorable subset. Withhold the connected sum and report the actual disposition category. Broader mixed-result treatment is outside this delivery. |
| Current affirmed relationship points to a missing/non-current source | Family closure still describes current Box 1 sources, but the affirmed relationship is a current orphan and must block connected readiness; do not erase the relationship claim or relabel the family closed-empty. |

P2 found a workable content shape: per-report bridge rules use the real `subject`/`joined` declarations and scoped status content (accepted SLI `rule.sli-answer-status-*` pattern), then a non-collecting readiness selection reads both report results and common-account results. Readiness must not collect Box 1. A single family-authorized collector then publishes line8a. The P2 inactive second branch is not route-selection evidence and is excluded. Relationship states use concrete current fact carriers: affirmed, denied, cannot-tell/unresolved, or withdrawn. Missing carrier means missing support; it is not a negative answer. The two source reports are enumerated by the existing closed F1098 family, and each must have a current relationship outcome with the same borrowing key; no extra completeness assertion is proposed.

For the supported candidate boundary, each report keeps its own Box 1 and nine answers; each report's Box 2 is checked against the common numeric ceiling; raw Box 3 dates remain report-local and must agree before one borrowing-wide debt category is used. The conservative existing $750,000 / $375,000 MFS limit and exclusion for origination on or before 1987-10-13 remain; no higher pre-2017 limit or refinance treatment is introduced. The actual line8a amount is the sum of authorized current family Box 1 members once readiness succeeds. No allocation or interest-apportionment formula is proposed.

**Clear-account exit is unresolved.** There is no demonstrated current bounded mechanism that lets a person exit a connected account while retaining report identities and safely selecting singleton treatment. Until the owner dispositions that limitation, once connected-route evidence exists the system must fail closed on its withdrawal or incompleteness; it must not silently return to a favorable singleton. A future explicit exit would need to establish that current report membership has been withdrawn/cleared and that the remaining singleton set is complete, without changing the meaning of “same borrowing.” This proposal does not choose that policy.

### Lifecycle and correction consequences

| Event | Required current-state effect |
| --- | --- |
| Correct Box 1 amount only; report identity and membership unchanged | Use the source recorder's same-report correction lineage. Recompute that report's reason and readiness/line8a; keep the same-borrowing assertion, membership and numeric ceiling current because their propositions and keys did not change. Keep other reports' answers current. Group explanation associates the corrected source finding with its true current revision. |
| Correct a report-local ordinary answer only | Replace only that answer on that exact report; recompute that report's result and downstream readiness. Equal answers on peers do not change. |
| Correct/withdraw continuing-borrowing claim | End or replace borrowing-year support and dependent derived readiness/amount results. Preserve independent raw membership claims as recorded; without current borrowing recognition they cannot support calculation. A withdrawal is neither a contrary answer nor permission to restore singleton. |
| Correct/withdraw one report membership | Change only that pair's current outcome and dependent connected results. If connected evidence still selects the route, recompute or block it; do not treat the changed link as a clear-account decision. The exit policy remains owner-held. |
| Correct annual ceiling amount, without changing account scope | Replace that borrowing/year amount; re-evaluate all report Box 2 comparisons and limit readiness. The reported Box 2 observations stay untouched. |
| Change or withdraw the borrowing identity assertion | End dependent derived membership/readiness/amount results. Preserve independent raw membership findings as recorded; they remain unusable without a current recognized borrowing. An explicit relationship correction/withdrawal changes only the named pair's current outcome. |
| A family member is added after closure (including a late report) | Existing source-set lifecycle makes old closure-dependent outputs non-current. Record the new report source, its nine answers and its exact link status, reclose, then recompute both report results and readiness from all current family members. The family closure plus current per-report membership outcomes are the candidate completeness/account-coverage authority; add no separate report-set assertion. A same-borrowing/year ceiling remains current unless its subject/period changes or contrary evidence arrives. |
| Remove/re-record a report | Existing source removal/replacement and family membership lifecycle govern its Box 1 history. Re-recording is a new/current report only after its source and report-local answers are current; old account membership does not attach by similar labels or values. Reconfirm the exact report link. |
| Withdraw shared account support | The affected connected claim becomes absent/withdrawn; dependent result and amount are blocked. No prior positive result survives as current, and no singleton fallback occurs. |

These are required effects, not demonstrated mortgage-recorder behavior. The current mortgage answer recorder handles exact-report answer correction and withdrawal only; it does not record opaque mortgage borrowing identities, report membership, numeric ceilings, account-level lifecycle or route choice.
