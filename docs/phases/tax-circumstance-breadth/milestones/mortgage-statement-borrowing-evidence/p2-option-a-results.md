## Finding and evidence ceiling

The content-path experiment demonstrates that nine ordinary answers can remain
report-local while content reads per-report statuses, reconciles the common
borrowing ceiling, refuses the five isolated contrary/missing conditions, and
derives the correct summed line8a value through the authorized family
collector. The origination disagreement is unresolved because the marshaller
does not bind one scalar Box 3 when report assertions differ; no raw
“dates-agree” assertion or selected date was supplied.

The verified end-to-end publication surface remains limited by the current
mortgage presentation projector's singleton-statement assumption: it rejects
the correctly two-statement-pinned baseline line8a result. Therefore P2 does
not establish a durable baseline report/presentation despite the successful
in-memory derivation and completed record. This is a concrete presentation
boundary, not an evaluator/content-language gap. No projector or engine change
is authorized by this experiment.

Evidence is synthetic only. It does not establish obtained user input,
recorder behavior, or a group reader.
