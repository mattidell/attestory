## Observations

- The two iterations converge on the need for a closure-backed zero to pin a declared closure finding rather than rely on an unpinned empty collection. It2 demonstrates the pin against the shipped runners; it1 supplies broader hand-authored scenario and schema evidence.
- The iterations also converge on first-class form-field content, inert source citations, strict schema rejection, and immutable tax-year evolution. It2 reduces the number of new families by using the kernel fact-type family and rule membership for box meaning. That reduction leaves citation attachment and source-instance collision pressure less explicit, not resolved by the passing harness.
- The stale projection result establishes a narrow safety property: a stale closure projection alone cannot publish line 2b. It does not establish that coverage itself is a record-derived projection.
- The closure-as-elective-fact move does not introduce T1 or T2 doctrine. Its basis and act lineage remain represented through runner input pins rather than committed closure finding and process-record fixtures.

## Dissent

I dissent from examination-it2's unqualified Q7, Q8, and Q9 conclusions. Q7 is not a full Article 9 pass while citation resolution depends on `citation_resolved` outside the schema. Q8 is not a full Article 11/F8 pass while required citations are not attached to the fact types, rules, or parameters and tax-year mutation is not tested. Q9 is not established while coverage ignores `RunResult` and no process record is exhibited. I do not dissent from the reported dual-runner parity, the closure-finding pin, the no-document identity shape, or the reserved-entry safety result.
