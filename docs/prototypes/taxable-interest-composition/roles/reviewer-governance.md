# Role: Governance Reviewer — Taxable-Interest Composition (Committee)

Medium tier. Owner-launched external context (2026-07-14). Committee round over **two independent designs** of the same two propositions: the incumbent (`it1/`) and the clean-room rival (`it2/`). Measure both and make their convergence and divergence explicit — that comparison is the point of the round.

**Independence exclusions — do not read:** the Adversary reviewer's output (`reviews/round-1-adversary.md`), any draft or notes toward ADR-0026. There is no prior TIC review to exclude.

**Read:** the topic `plan.md`, `charter-it1.md`, `charter-it2.md`, `it1/design.md`, `examination-it1.md`, `it2/design.md`, `examination-it2.md`, `docs/governance/`, ratified ADRs 0002, 0004, 0006–0012, 0014, 0015, **0016**, 0017, and committed `packages/derivation/`, `packages/kernel/`, and the Source-Completeness b1-subtotal/source-family/horizon content. The inert `taxable-interest-composition-spike.md` and ADR-0021 may be read as superseded prior art.

**Assignment.** For **each design**, measure both propositions against the governance set:

- **TIC-P1 (coextensive universe + declaration).** Does the composition satisfy ADR-0016 decision 4 (an *explicit composition established as coextensive*), decision 5 (no box-1 promotion to line-2b authority), and decision 3 (coverage never reports a broader universe complete from a narrow closure)? Judge the **narrow-substitution defeat** concretely: the incumbent's validator V5 (line-2b rule must `ref` every constituent subtotal) vs the rival's slot-bijection validator + `require_closed` operation — is each sufficient, and does either leave a ref-subset or coverage hole?
- **The boundary divergence — you must rule on it.** The incumbent's universe is `{B1, B3, non-form}` with taxable OID **deferred** to versioned-surface growth; the rival's is `{B1, B3, taxable-OID, unreported}` with OID a **mandatory** constituent, on the ground that the Form 1040 line-2b instructions name Form 1099-OID. State plainly whether a composition that **omits taxable OID** can honestly claim coextensiveness with Form 1040 line 2b under ADR-0016 decision 4, or whether the incumbent's *scoped/versioned* coextensiveness claim ("complete under the constituents I chose") is the very narrow-promotion decision 5 forbids, dressed as a composition. This is the round's central question.
- **Binding mechanism.** The incumbent binds composition to line-rule by package membership (a new `composition` pin role flagged optional); the rival adds a required `composition:{id,version}` pin on the line rule plus a generic `require_closed` operation. Judge each against ADR-0010's closed pin-role set and Article-7 edge integrity — does either introduce an unlicensed pin role, a new standing-affecting edge, or runner-resident tax meaning?
- **TIC-P2 (honest zero + lifecycle).** Confirm the empty zero requires every constituent's current-horizon literal-true closure (never box-1 alone), and the late-member lifecycle displaces through ADR-0017 individuation + ADR-0010 derivation edges only, with no new edge and no resurrection of the old zero.

Classify every finding (decision-blocking / production condition / non-blocking). Do not repair designs. Where the two designs converge on a mechanism, say so — convergence under independent authorship is the strongest signal available.

**Output:** `reviews/round-1-governance.md`, findings labeled TIC-G1, TIC-G2, …, each naming the design(s) it applies to, ending with a verdict **per proposition per design** (accept / conditionally accept (conditions listed) / reject) and a one-line recommendation on which design's boundary and mechanism to carry into ADR-0026 (or a hybrid, naming the parts). Advisory: the owner decides disposition.
