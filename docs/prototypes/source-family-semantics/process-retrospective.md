# Source-Family Semantics Process Retrospective

Date: 2026-07-12. Status: complete; ADR-0016 ratified.

## Outcome

Two paper rivals plus one repair settled the semantic boundary cheaply:

- exact claim + canonical predicate carry family meaning;
- a narrow closed family authorizes only its matching subtotal;
- broader results require identical or explicitly coextensive composition.

The adversary review prevented a false conclusion by finding consistent
mislabeling and the undefined late-member trigger. The repair closed the former
and exposed the latter as a separate Tier-3 machinery decision.

## Process assessment

- Paper was sufficient for SFS-P1/P2; no evaluator was built.
- Proposition-specific evidence depth worked: SFS-P1/P2 concluded while SFS-P3
  escalated rather than forcing the whole iteration deeper.
- Removing review line caps improved the round-2 reports; both fully documented
  evidence without compression work.
- One governance review used the wrong repository path; foreman moved it
  mechanically without altering content.
- The original papers assumed manual closure withdrawal. Requiring a complete
  late-discovery lifecycle exposed the missing currency mechanism before schema
  or runner work began.

## Carry-forward

- State the event that changes currency, not only the desired before/after
  states.
- For standing-affecting machinery, map every effect to derivation or
  individuation edges and stop if a third relation appears.
- Do not use “record-derived” as a synonym for authorized; identify what record
  facts and edges grant the projection meaning.
- Keep Medium-tier reviews with explicit measurements and no line cap.
