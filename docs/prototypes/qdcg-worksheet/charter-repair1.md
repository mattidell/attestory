## Required repair

Produce one paper design and examination for the converged successor posture:

1. One versioned successor producer owns line 16. It may not use dual
   line-16 producers, `conflict_semantics`, first-publisher ordering, or a
   claim that the package validates guard exclusivity.
2. When qualified dividends are zero, the successor reaches the existing
   ordinary computation without requiring either capital-gain declaration.
   The reduction property must be shown by algebra and the expression's lazy
   evaluation must be cited from committed machinery.
3. When qualified dividends are positive, both categorical declarations are
   factually required before the worksheet can publish. Their evaluated access
   must create the displacement pins; `requires`-only language is insufficient.
4. A present `yes` answer must have an honest, walkable outcome. The design may
   use only a committed disposition behavior, or it must name a versioned
   record/walk vocabulary change as an unimplemented production condition. It
   must never claim a false guard emits a custom blocked code.
5. Preserve the settled D2-P3 boundary: declarations of `no` and the
   `CAPITAL_GAIN_DISTRIBUTION_RECORDED` signal are mutually excluded at
   admission before mutation, in both temporal orders and in one batch. The
   worksheet must not read box 2a, the signal, or recorded-non-composable
   content.

## Cases and outputs

The design retests these bounded cases with synthetic `demo-*` values only:

- qualified zero with declarations absent → ordinary result publishes;
- qualified positive with declarations absent → factual non-publication,
  naming both contributable declarations;

  with access-log displacement path shown;
- each present `yes` outcome → honestly described and walkable;
- declaration/signal contradiction in declaration-first, signal-first, and
  same-batch orders → admission fails closed; and
- attempted direct worksheet read of box 2a/signal → unrepresentable.

`examination-repair1.md` (≤80 lines). The examination must identify D2-P1,
